# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plan must be able to move resources to an unaffected region

As said in [[cloud-providers-have-power-backups-and-redundancy-systems-to-minimise-impact-of-power-outages]], a [[zettelkasten-for-work/zettelkasten/permanent/business-continuity-and-disaster-recovery-plan]] must be able to replicate application resources to an unaffected region.

This is in the event that [[zettelkasten-for-work/zettelkasten/permanent/natural-disasters-can-affect-application-availability-and-data]].

In a _Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plan_, what must cloud providers be able to do **in the case of a natural disaster**? :: Replicate application resources to an unaffected region.^1738532573082
