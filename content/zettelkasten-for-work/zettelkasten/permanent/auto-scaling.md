# Auto scaling

Auto scaling refers to high [[elasticity-of-scalability]] that [[cloud-computing]] usually offers, of which refers to [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/infrastructure-as-a-service]] being scaled automatically according to a set of conditions (such as [[zettelkasten-for-work/zettelkasten/permanent/scale-systems-in-the-direction-of-demand]]).

There are 2 types of auto-scaling:

- [[dynamic-scaling]]
- [[predictive-scaling]]
