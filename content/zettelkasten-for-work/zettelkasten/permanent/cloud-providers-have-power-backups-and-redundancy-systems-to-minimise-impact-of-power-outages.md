# Cloud providers have power backups and redundancy systems to minimise impact of power outages

As [[cloud-providers-only-guarantee-availability-for-systems-they-cover]], [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]] invest heavily in battery-operated power backups and other redundant systems as [[power-outages-can-cause-a-lack-of-availability]].
