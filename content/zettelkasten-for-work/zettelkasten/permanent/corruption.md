# Corruption

Corruption occurs when individuals in positions of power abuse their authority for advantages, like these [[zettelkasten-for-work/zettelkasten/permanent/examples-of-corruption]], and involves at least two parties: the abuser and the person who hopes to gain an advantage from the abuse. [[corruption-must-be-intentional-and-advantageous-to-involved-parties]] to be considered corruption.
