# Cloud providers offer governance features and services to control access of resources

[[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]] will offer [[application-governance]] features and services to control who has access of resources and what level of access they have (authentication and authorisation).

It can also be applied to users using resources such that they may be prevented from creating resources that carry significant cost.
