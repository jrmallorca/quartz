# Cloud computing is as scalable as the company wants

Unlike buying on-premises servers, the company can ensure their [[scalability-of-systems]] by buying only as much infrastructure they need from a [[cloud-provider]]. For example, if they don't have as much traffic or data, then they don't need as big of a storage capacity.

This is possible through the [[shared-responsibility-model]] and the [[consumption-based-model]].
