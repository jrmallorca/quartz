# Bribery

Bribery is when something of value is exchanged to influence the recipient as [[bribery-results-in-preferential-treatment]] for the offerer. [[bribery-is-often-disguised]] so it may be hard to detect, but there are [[zettelkasten-for-work/zettelkasten/permanent/signs-of-bribery]] that could indicate it.
