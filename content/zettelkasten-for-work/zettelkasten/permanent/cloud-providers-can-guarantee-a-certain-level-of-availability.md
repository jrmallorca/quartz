# Cloud providers can guarantee a certain level of availability

[[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]] offer a service-level agreement (SLA) that guarantees a certain level of availability as a percentage that is usually close to 100%.

This means that [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]] will be responsible for minimising [[common-issues-of-losing-application-and-data-availability]].
