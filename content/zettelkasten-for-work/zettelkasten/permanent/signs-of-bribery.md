# Signs of bribery

The following could be signs that someone is receiving bribes:

- Continued acceptance of poor quality work.
- Purchase of unnecessary, inappropriate, large, unrequired orders.

The following could be signs that someone is funding bribes:

- Unsupported and unprofessional invoices.
