# Cloud spot virtual machines are ideal for flexible or interruptible workloads

Cloud [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/infrastructure-as-a-service]] [[spot-virtual-machine]]s are ideal for workloads that can withstand interruption, or have flexible start and end times.

This may be a background processing job that can start and stop as needed.
