# Habit - Speak from the diaphragm

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- During speaking.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Sit or stand comfortably with correct posture.
2. Slowly breathe in through the diaphragm.
3. Speak a sentence.
4. Repeat the process.
