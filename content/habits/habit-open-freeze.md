# Habit - Open freeze

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Freezing multiples of the same ingredient together.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Place ingredients on a tray separately from each other.
2. Once frozen, move ingredients to an appropriate container.
