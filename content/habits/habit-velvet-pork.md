# Habit - Velvet pork

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Before stir frying pork.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

### Ingredients

- 1 tbsp of water.
- 1.5 tsp of Shaoxing wine.
- 1.5 tsp of oyster sauce.
- (Optional) 0.375 tsp of baking soda

- Sliced pork.

- 1.5 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp oil
- (Optional) 0.25 tsp sesame oil
- (Optional) 0.125 tsp cracked pepper

### Instructions

1. Cut pork into thinner slices as required for the recipe.
2. In a bowl that can contain the pork, add the following:
  - 1 tbsp of water.
  - 1.5 tsp of Shaoxing wine.
  - 1.5 tsp of oyster sauce.
  - (Optional) 0.375 tsp of baking soda
  - Sliced pork.
3. With your hands, mix everything in the bowl until the pork is well coated.
4. Add the following ingredients into the bowl:
  - 1.5 tsp cornstarch
  - 1 tsp oil
  - (Optional) 0.25 tsp sesame oil
  - (Optional) 0.125 tsp cracked pepper
5. With your hands, mix everything in the bowl until the pork is well coated.
6. Marinate for 30 minutes up to overnight.
