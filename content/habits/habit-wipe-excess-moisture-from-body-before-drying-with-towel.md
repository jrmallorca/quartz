# Habit - Wipe excess moisture from body before drying with towel

Wiping the excess moisture from the body will reduce the water required to be absorbed by the towel for each subsequent use. This will prolong the life of the towel.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After washing yourself.
- Before drying with a towel.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

Like a squeegee, use your hands to wipe excess moisture from all parts of the body.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Satisfaction from reduced wetness.
