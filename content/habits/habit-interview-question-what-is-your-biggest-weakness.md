# Habit - Interview question: "What is your biggest weakness?"

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

When asked a question along the lines of "What is your biggest weakness?".

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Avoid “I’m a perfectionist” type answers; avoid weaknesses that are actually strengths.
- Keep weaknesses strictly about business; no personal weaknesses.
- Do not give out a weakness that would be purely disadvantageous for us.
    - E.g. if you’re not punctual, do not mention that.
- Identify a weakness that isn’t a major requirement in your day-to-day job.
    - E.g. Not having great presentation skills might be alright for a role which doesn’t require much customer contact.

### Potential weaknesses:

- Initially shy but have actively tried to become more outspoken.
  - [[story-presented-benefits-of-vim-motion-to-company]]
  - [[story-increased-adoption-rate-of-az900]]
- Anything from the "Desirable skills" in the job description.
  - [[story-created-replacement-of-expensive-api-for-addressing]] shows how we can quickly learn skills.
