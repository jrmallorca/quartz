# Habit - Replace dustbag in Roborock Qrevo S

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

A month after dustbag is replaced.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. On the home base of the vacuum, take off plastic cover located on top of the robot vacuum.
2. Pull the grey seal at the right until the limit is reached.
3. Pull out the dust bag.
4. (Optional) Check how full the dust bag is.
5. Insert a new, or current, dustbag and place its grey seal inside its section.
6. Put plastic cover back on.
