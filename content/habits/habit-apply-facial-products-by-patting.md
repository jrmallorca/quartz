# Habit - Apply facial products by patting

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After showering.
- After a face wash.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Apply the product on the fingertips of one of your hands.
2. Briefly spread the product to the other hand's fingertips.
3. Repeatedly pat the product on the skin in an upwards motion.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Greater absorption of products to the skin.
- Relief from not feeling seborrhoeic dermatitis.
