# Habit - Season wok

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

1. Wok is made of carbon steel.
2. After wok has been tempered.
3. Wok has visibly bare material without polymerised fats.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Heat the wok to medium-high heat until slightly smoking.
2. Turn off heat.
3. Add a teaspoon of oil to the wok.
4. With a clean kitchen towel, wipe the oil all around the inside of the wok.
5. With another clean kitchen towel, attempt to wipe the oil off the wok.
