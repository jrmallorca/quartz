# Habit - Third-person debugging

Third-person debugging is a method for analysing personal problems by imagining a friend, who has the exact same issue, coming to you for guidance. This positions us to a third-person role where advice and solutions come from a more unbiased perspective.

This is similar to the [[rubber-ducky-debugging]] as it makes us explain our problems which can induce realisations of potential solutions.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Experiencing a problem.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Imagine a friend with the exact same problem coming to you for guidance.
- Give an unbiased perspective of the problem and lay out any observations and possible solutions.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- More control over the problem.
- Realisations and possible solutions.
