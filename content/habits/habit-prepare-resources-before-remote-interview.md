# Habit - Prepare resources before remote interview

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Before the start of a remote interview.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Open your CV.
- Open the job description.
- Open a file which contains:
  - Details about the company and/or interview.
  - Questions to ask the interviewers.
  - A blank space to take notes.
