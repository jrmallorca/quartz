# Habit - Cable/banded lateral raise

Shoulders should be exercised with lower weight, higher sets, and higher amount of reps. For example, 4 sets of 20 reps.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- [[upper-body-exercise]]
- Exercising side deltoids.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Set up anchor around hip level and attach the band.
  - This is because side deltoids are most stretched at the 90 degree angle.
- Spread the feet shoulder-width apart.
- Slightly lean forward.
- Hold the handle with a [[neutral-grip]] in front of your groin.
  - Ensure there's enough distance such that the cable is under tension.
  - Experiment with tightly gripping and loosely gripping.
- With the other hand, either hold the pole of the cable machine or put it on top of its respective thigh.
- Raise your arm, leading with your elbow, until it's parallel to the floor.
  - Ensure the hands are never above the elbow.
- Hold the top position for 1 second.
- Slowly lower the arm back to the resting position.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Endorphin release.
