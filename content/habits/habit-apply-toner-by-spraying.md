# Habit - Apply toner by spraying

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After showering.
- After a face wash.
- Before applying [[moisturiser]]

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. If toner is not in a spray bottle, decant the toner into a spray bottle.
1. Close eyes.
2. Spray toner evenly across face.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Greater absorption of products to the skin.
- Relief from not feeling seborrhoeic dermatitis.
