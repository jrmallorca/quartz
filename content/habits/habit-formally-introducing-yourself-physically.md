# Habit - Formally introducing yourself physically

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Physically meeting someone new in a professional context.
- Presenting in a professional context.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Smile.
2. Say "Hi".
3. Face the audience with your toes, torso, and head.
4. Use [[articulation]] to state your full name.
5. (Optionally) Indicate a handshake.
   1. Reach your hand out.
   2. Have your fingers extended outward.
   3. Tilt your hand 45° outward.
6. Say a statement with positivity.
7. State your role or title.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Make a good first impression.
- [[halo-effect]]
