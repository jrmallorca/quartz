# Habit - Lockpick luggage dial lock

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Forgetting dial lock combination.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

For each number dial:

1. Observe the shine of the metal underneath the dial.
2. Turn the number dial until the gap is located, i.e. the absence of the shine.

Until the luggage lock is open:

1. Turn all number dials counter-clockwise once.
2. Attempt to push down the button to check if it's unlocked.
