# Habit - Pan fry salmon fillet

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Cooking salmon fillet

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Defrost the fillet closer to room temperature.
2. [[habit-wait-for-leidenfrost-effect-to-occur-before-cooking-on-stainless-steel-cookware]]
3. With a clean kitchen towel, pat dry the salmon on all sides.
4. Salt all sides of the salmon.
5. Place the salmon into the pan, skin-side down.
6. Wait for 5 minutes.
7. Flip the salmon and wait for 2 minutes.
8. Sear each side for 10 seconds.
9. Serve.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Satisfying salmon to eat.
