# Habit - Remove chicken skin from drumsticks

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Making poached chicken.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Get a paper towel.
2. Rip it in half.
3. Use half of the paper towel to hold the tail of the chicken drumstick.
4. Use the other half of the paper towel to deskin the chicken drumstick from the head.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Skinless drumsticks.
- Chicken skin.
