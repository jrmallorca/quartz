# Habit - Prepare salmon for sushi and/or sashimi

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After [[habit-selecting-salmon-to-eat-raw-from-a-market]].
- Before [[cutting-salmon-fillets-into-sushi-and-sashimi]].

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. If cutting a whole salmon, then cut each fillet 4 finger widths each (saku blocks).
2. Make a dry brine by mixing 3 tablespoons of salt and 2 tablespoons of sugar together.
3. For each fillet:
  1. Pat dry with a clean kitchen towel.
  2. Season with the dry brine.
4. Place all fillets into a sealed container.
5. Refrigerate for 12 hours.
6. Take out fillet container.
7. Remove liquid from container.
8. Flip all fillets.
9. Refrigerate for 12 hours.
10. Take out fillet container.
11. For each fillet:
  1. Wash off brine under cold water.
  2. Pat dry with a clean kitchen towel.
  3. Wrap tightly with cling film.
  4. Wrap tightly with cling film again.
12. Freeze the fillets at -20°C for 7 days.
