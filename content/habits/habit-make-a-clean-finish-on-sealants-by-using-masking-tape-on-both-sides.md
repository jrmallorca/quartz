# Habit - Make a clean finish on sealant application

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- [[detect-water-based-damage-due-to-worn-out-sealants]]
- Observation of possible leaking due to current sealant.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Apply masking tape on both sides of where sealant is being applied, leaving a gap for the sealant.
2. Apply the sealant in between the gap of the masking tapes.
3. Smooth out the sealant by running your finger across the sealed edge.
4. Before the sealant dries, remove all tape.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Improved aesthetic of sealant application.
