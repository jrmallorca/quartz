# Habit - Air dry face after washing yourself

We can simplify the skincare routine a bit by foregoing the face towel and letting the face air dry. While this will take a bit longer to dry the face, [[humectant]]s can take advantage of all the moisture.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After washing yourself

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Gently wipe off excess water from the face.
2. (Optionally) wait for the face to dry.
3. Apply skincare routine.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Better moisture retention for the face.
- Better face aesthetic.
