# Habit - Squeegee walls after shower

Wiping the walls after a shower keeps it clean and prevents soap scum, mold, mildew, etc. from sticking to the walls.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After washing yourself.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Get the squeegee.
- For each shower wall, wipe down water from top to bottom with the squeegee.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Satisfaction of a cleaner looking shower.
