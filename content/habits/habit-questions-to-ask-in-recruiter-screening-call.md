# Habit - Questions to ask in recruiter screening call

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Initial call from a recruiter telling you about the job. This is NOT the interview stage.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

### If not given from the posting

- What's the salary range?

### During or after they're finished

- What's the company culture like?
- Why has this position become available?
  - If new, why was it created?
  - If backfill, why is it open?
- What are the day to day tasks someone in this role might do?

### If you feel like you can ask more

- What kind of benefits does your company have?
- Do they have self development or training programs?
