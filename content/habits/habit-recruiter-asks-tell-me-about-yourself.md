# Habit - Recruiter asks "Tell me about yourself?"

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After being asked "Tell me about yourself?" from a recruiter.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

Based off your [[explanation-of-cv]]:

1. Choose your starting point, based on your years of experience.
  - As we have 3 years of experience, start with the moment you graduated and walk through your employment experience since then.
2. Highlight your skills.
  - This should be relevant to the position you're looking to take.
3. Highlight your promotions and/or achievements.
  - This should be relevant to the position you're looking to take.
4. State why you want to move from your current job to the new opportunity.
  - Bring them up to speed on your current situation. Why you wanted to apply for their job, what you’re looking to do next, etc.

> 1. I graduated with my degree in Computer Science 3 years ago and immediately went to work as a software engineer for a medium/large IT consultancy BJSS.
> 2. I'm knowledgeable in Java, Flutter, Linux, and a whole other range of tools and frameworks.
>
> Since I've been employed, I've
>   - reduce the entire mainframe migration effort by 25% by leading the development on a suite of automating legacy code through a Java application,
>   - avoided our client paying £700,000, within 5 weeks of onboarding with another Java application,
>   - and helped out testers by taking on the effort of introducing automated testing to our client.
> 3. Although I enjoy my team and work with my company, I'd like to take on more challenging roles that would allow me to develop new skills and polish existing ones.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Potential for another job.
