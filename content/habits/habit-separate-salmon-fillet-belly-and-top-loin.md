# Habit - Separate salmon fillet belly and top loin

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

During [[habit-cutting-salmon-fillets-into-sushi-and-sashimi]]

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Observe the thick white line halfway through the fillet, separating the belly and the top loin.
2. A millimetre away from the white line, cut the top loin away.
  1. If the fillet has skin on, start to curve a millimetre away from the skin and cut parallel to the skin to remove it.
3. A millimetre away from the white line, cut the belly away.
  1. If the fillet has skin on, start to curve a millimetre away from the skin and cut parallel to the skin to remove it.
