# Habit - Recruiter asks salary expectations

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

When the recruiter asks about salary expectations.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

### If the salary isn't displayed in the job posting

- Deflect back with "What's the salary budget range?".

### If the salary is displayed in the job posting or given when you asked

- If happy, tell them it would take the higher end of the range for you to move.
- If unhappy, say your lower limit.
- Ask for their benefits if they haven't said it yet.

### If not given the salary budget range AFTER asking

- Say "I would like to know more about what the role entails before I give my range".
- Ask questions from [[habit-questions-to-ask-in-recruiter-screening-call]]
- Ask again "What's the salary budget range?".
