# Habit - Wait for Leidenfrost effect to occur before cooking on stainless steel cookware

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Before putting oil into the stainless steel cookware.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Turn on stove.
- Put stainless steel on pan.
- Wait for 1 minute.
- Splash a few droplets of water to observe [[leidenfrost-effect]].
- If the [[leidenfrost-effect]] is observed, start adding oil to the pan.
- Otherwise, repeat the process of waiting and observing the [[leidenfrost-effect]].

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Lack of annoyance from food sticking.
- Satisfaction of food not sticking.
