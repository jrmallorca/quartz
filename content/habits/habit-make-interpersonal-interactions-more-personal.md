# Habit - Make interpersonal interactions more personal

[[the-impression-left-after-interpersonal-interaction-are-more-important-than-details-of-the-interaction]]. Making the interaction more personal would leave a better impression. For example, when someone's birthday happens, do you think they'd be happier with a comment on their post or a handwritten card?

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Before a potential interaction, in-person or not (e.g., text or call).
- Before or during an important event/milestone for a person.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

Think of alternative methods to make the potential interaction more personal.

Take someone's birthday as an example:

- Send a handwritten card and gifts to them.
- Call or message them directly.
- If invited, attend their birthday party.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

Fulfilment with life.
