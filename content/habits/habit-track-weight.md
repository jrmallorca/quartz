# Habit - Track weight

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After waking up.
- After going toilet.
- Before eating.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Remove internal urine and stool.
- Strip off all clothing.
- Weigh yourself on the weighing machine.
- Record the weight onto a tracker.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Visual observation of possible improvements.
