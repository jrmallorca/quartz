# Habit - Making matcha tea

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Ready equipment:
	1. Matcha bowl (chawan).
	2. Whisk (chasu).
	3. Sieve.
	4. Teaspoon (chashaku).
	5. Kettle.
	6. Matcha powder.
2. Boil at least 100ml of water.
3. Pour hot water into bowl, saving ~70ml in the kettle for later.
4. Soak the tines of the whisk into the bowl of hot water for 60 seconds.
5. Remove whisk and either put it back on its stand or let it stand on its handle.
6. Remove hot water and dry bowl with cloth.
7. Place sieve on top of bowl.
8. Add a heaped teaspoon of matcha powder into the sieve.
9. Sift the powder into the bowl.
10. Add ~70ml of hot, not boiling (70°C-80°C), water into the bowl.
11. Slowly circle the whisk inside the perimeter of the bowl to gather the powder.
12. With your wrist, vigorously whisk the inside of the bowl in a back-and-forth motion. With your elbow, slowly move side-to-side while whisking. Do this for 15-30 seconds until a fine foam is observed.
13. Remove whisk and rinse it.
14. Serve.
