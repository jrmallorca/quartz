# Habit - Remove obstacles before painting interior walls

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Before preparing or painting interior walls.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Remove any electrical cover plates, if possible.
- Tape over any electricals.
- Remove furniture, if possible.
- Add a cover (tarp) on the floor and furniture.
