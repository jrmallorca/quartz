# Habit - Update recently painted mid-sheen surfaces to matt

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

The walls have been painted over relatively recently with mid sheen paint and you want to paint it to matt.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Prepare the surface for painting through [[habit-painting-interior-surfaces]] or [[painting-ceilings]].
2. Paint the surface with a sheen lower than the original paint, but not matt.
3. Paint it with your desired matt paint.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Visual observation of solving an unusual painting problem.
- Desired aesthetic.
