# Habit - Key surfaces before painting

Keying surfaces will create a more adherent surface for primer or paint.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After [[habit-scrape-and-fill-interior-surfaces-before-painting]].
- Before [[prime-surfaces-before-painting-when-it-is-either-dark-has-sheen-or-has-visibly-unpainted-parts]].
- An hour, or however long it takes for the primer/paint to dry, and before [[habit-painting-interior-surfaces]].
- Surface has sheen and will paint the surface with a lower sheen.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Get fine-grit sandpaper.
- For walls and ceilings, attach sandpaper to a pole sander.
- [[keying-in-terms-of-painting]]

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Satisfaction of a feeling a smooth surface.
- Satisfaction of looking at a professional job.
