# Habit - Interview question: "Why do you want to leave your job?"

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

When asked a question along the lines of "Why do you want to leave your job?".

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

Never badmouth the company.

Respond with a desire to grow your career and your skills.

For example:

> I'm grateful for my current job providing me with an environment to grow, especially with such great people. Unfortunately, my company is being acquired. I'm looking for somewhere that could provide me with that same environment. Being a consultancy, I'll have a chance to encounter different projects and learn from a variety of people and experiences.
