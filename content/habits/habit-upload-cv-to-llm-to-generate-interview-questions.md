# Habit - Upload CV to LLM to generate interview questions

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Preparing for an interview.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Create a copy of the CV where personal details are anonymised/deleted.
- Upload the CV to a LLM.
- Prompt the LLM to generate STAR format questions and their respective answers based off the details in the CV.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Relief from being more prepared for an interview.

## Related content

On [[2025-03-17]], I quickly had to generate a few STAR format questions to help prepare Mika for a phone interview. I uploaded her CV, after anonymising the details, to use it to generate answers from it.
