# Habit - Apply sugar soap on interior surfaces

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Before [[prime-surfaces-before-painting-when-it-is-either-dark-has-sheen-or-has-visibly-unpainted-parts]].
- After [[habit-key-surfaces-before-painting]].

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Fill an empty bucket with sugar soap and warm water.
- Wipe the walls bottom-to-top with a damp sponge/rag.
- After 1 minute, fill an empty bucket with warm water.
- Wipe the walls top-to-bottom with a damp sponge/rag with only warm water.
