# Habit - Unbutton clothes before laundry

Unbuttoning clothes will reduce the likelihood of both snagging and stressing the garment.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Before washing clothes.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

Unbutton every applicable clothing before washing.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

Maintenance of clothes.
