# Habit - Get rid of a stitch when running

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

When a stitch happens.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Slow down your activity.
2. Improve posture.
3. Regulate breathing.
4. Inhale deeply into the stomach and exhale fully.
