# Habit - Informally introducing yourself physically

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Physically meeting someone new in a casual context.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Smile.
2. Say "Hey".
3. Face the audience with your toes, torso, and head.
4. Use [[articulation]] to state your first name.
5. (Optionally) Indicate a hug.
    1. From the front, rotate both shoulders 45° outwards.
    2. Extend your arms until the elbow angle is a bit more than 90°.
    3. Have your fingers extended outward.
6. Say a statement with positivity.
7. State your role or title.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Make a good first impression.
- [[halo-effect]]
