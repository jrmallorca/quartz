# Habit - Selecting salmon to eat raw from a market

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Before preparing salmon for sushi and/or sashimi.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

In short, get farmed Atlantic/Norwegian salmon with the least difference in time between farm to market.

Otherwise:

- Ensure salmon is farmed, not wild.
- Ensure salmon is not smelly.
- Ensure salmon has a small difference in time between farm to market.
- Ask if salmon is flash frozen (frozen in -31°C for a day).
