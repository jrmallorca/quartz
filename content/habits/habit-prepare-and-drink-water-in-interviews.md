# Habit - Prepare and drink water in interviews

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Alternative method of stalling an interview question.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Prepare a bottle or cup of water before the interview.
2. After an interview question, SLOWLY drink a bit of water.
