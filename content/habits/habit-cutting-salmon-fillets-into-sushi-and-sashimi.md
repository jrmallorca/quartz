# Habit - Cutting salmon fillets into sushi and sashimi

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

After [[habit-prepare-salmon-for-sushi-and-or-sashimi]].

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. [[habit-separate-salmon-fillet-belly-and-top-loin]]
2. Take one of the salmon parts and observe the grain of the meat and prepare to cut against it.
3. From the thinnest to the thickest part of the salmon:
  4. Angle the blade such that it is almost parallel against the flesh.
  5. From the heel to the tip of knife, attempt to slice away 2mm-3mm thick piece in one motion.
  6. Softly fold the cut piece in half length-wise and place away from the cutting station.
