# Habit - Return mail to sender

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Mail has been received with your address but not for anyone living in the address.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. With a pen or pencil, put a cross through the address.
2. Write "Not known at this address".
3. Drop the mail into a postbox.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Reduced clutter.
- Removal of cue minimises cognition.
