# Bad habits are autocatalytic

Bad [[habit]]s undergo [[autocatalysis]], i.e., they cause the very feeling you try to numb.

Consider the following example:

1. Shaming obese people into weight loss can stress them out.
2. To de-stress, they start (over) eating.
3. By overeating, they get stressed again due to getting fatter.
4. To de-stress, they start (over) eating...

It causes the [[abstinence-violation-effect]].
