# Habit - Breathing pattern when jogging

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

During a jog.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Breathe deeply from the diaphragm.
- Use the 3:3 or 4:4 rhythm:
  - Continuously inhale for 3 - 4 strides.
  - Continuously exhale for 3 - 4 strides.
- [[habit-jogging-form]]
