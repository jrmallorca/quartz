# Habit - Freeze ginger

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- To make ginger into paste.
- Excess ginger to store in pantry.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Freeze ginger in a container in the freezer.
- Use it within 6 months.
