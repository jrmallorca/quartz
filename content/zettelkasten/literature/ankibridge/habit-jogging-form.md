# Habit - Jogging form

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Before or during a jog.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Straighten the spine and slightly lean.
2. Relax shoulders.
3. [[habit-breathing-pattern-when-jogging]]
4. Use a metronome to run 170 - 200 strides per minute.
5. With each step:
  1. Ensure the foot is parallel to the ground (midfoot strike).
  2. Ensure the knee is slightly flexed.
  3. Ensure the ankle is vertically in line with the knee.
