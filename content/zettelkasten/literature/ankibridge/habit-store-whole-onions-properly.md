# Habit - Store whole onions properly

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Store in a dark, cool, and dry place.
- Ensure it's well ventilated.
- Store in a cloth bag.
  - This contains helps to reduce clutter due to the skin falling off.
