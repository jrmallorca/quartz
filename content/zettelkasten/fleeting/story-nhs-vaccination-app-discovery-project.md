# Story - NHS vaccination app discovery project

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

## Situation - What was the situation/context?

For a month, one of my client's projects were to check the prospect of creating a vaccination app. Perhaps this was the result of me confiding in my senior that I wanted to improve my soft skills.

## Task - What was the task or challenge you had to deal with?

2 of the software engineer graduates came in for a technical perspective on this and present our findings.

Unfortunately, we weren't able to have access to code for the project. We would be interviewing a lot of the stakeholders of this public body.

## Action - What was the action you took?

Under the guidance of our business analyst, we interviewed quite a few teams and architects

## Result - What happened as a result of the action and what did you learn?

- Which projects/applications were similar to what they already wanted, NHS or external.
- Which services we think we would have to integrate with for the project.
- Any possible improvements that we could give value to the client, even if we never passed the discovery phase.

On a more personal level, I learned more of the business analyst role and improved my soft skills. I'm less afraid to just randomly go up to stakeholders to ask questions.
