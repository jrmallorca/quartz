# Habit - Ideal morning routine

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After waking up.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Immediately get feet on the ground, once awake.
2. Drink water.
3. Perform light exercise or stretch.
4. Shower with at least 30 seconds of cold water at the end.
5. 5 minutes of deep work.
6. In any order:
  1. Eat breakfast
  2. Go outside to a green space.
    - [[biophilia-hypothesis]]
  3. Practice gratitude.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

- Significantly increased energy output throughout the day.
