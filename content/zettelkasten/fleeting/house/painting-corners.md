# Painting corners

When using a paintbrush to paint corners, only fill the inside with paint. That means:

1. dip your brush in the paint,
2. scrape the sides of the brush against the inside lip of the container,
3. and scrape the bottom edge of the brush against the inside lip of the container.

Paint 1cm outside of the corner. Then paint right over the edge.

To remove brush marks, feather over it with a dry (or drier) brush. Or, use a mini roller right after painting but stay 1cm away from the corner.

Otherwise, you can tape the areas you don't want painted and use a 4 inch roller instead.
