1. Get either contract matt paint or an appropriate primer.
2. Attach paint roller to a pole.
3. [[cover-paint-tray-with-plastic-liner-for-easy-disposal]]
5. For each surface that is not primed yet:
    1. Roll paint roller into the paint and roll off excess paint through the grooves of the tray.
