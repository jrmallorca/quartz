1. Prime the walls, with cheap contract matt paint or actual primer, **only** if it satisfies any of the following:
    - The house is very old and you need an oil based primer.
    - You're transitioning from the dark or vivid colour to another colour.
    - It is new drywall with no paint in it.
    - It is wood with no paint in it.
[[prime-surfaces-before-painting-when-it-is-either-dark-has-sheen-or-has-visibly-unpainted-parts]]
2. After an hour, or however long it takes for the primer to dry, [[habit-key-surfaces-before-painting]]
3. Paint the walls.
    - [[types-of-paint-for-high-traffic-areas]]
    - [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLbUIevOxzY)
4. After an hour, or however long it takes for the paint to dry, [[habit-key-surfaces-before-painting]]
5. Paint the walls.
