# Painting ceilings

Painting ceilings is similar to [[habit-painting-interior-surfaces|painting house walls]].

The process to paint a ceiling:

1. Prepare the ceiling by scraping inconsistencies, filling in holes, sanding, and cleaning.
2. Roll the paint.
3. Cut in the paint to the ceiling (longest side of the wall or perpendicular to the direction of the sun).
4. Wait 1 hour.
5. Cut in the paint to the ceiling.
6. Sand the ceiling.
7. Roll the paint (in the opposite direction of the first coat)
   - Don't roll right into the corner.
   - Use gentle pressure.

If you're cutting in a ceiling, and have not painted the wall yet, you do not need to be careful.

With a 9 inch roller, have the metal of the paint roller at the "front" of the wet edge. This ensures that more pressure is applied to the front of whatever you're painting and the wet edge doesn't cause marks. This does not need to be considered with rollers between 12 - 18 inches.
