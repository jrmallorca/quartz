# Moneybox steps to make an offer

Our step-by-step guide to making an offer

- First, call or email the estate agent to tell them you want to make an offer.
- They’ll send you their offer template - this is usually an online form.
- Fill out the key details, including the price you’re offering to pay, how much you’re putting down as a deposit, plus the names of your Moneybox mortgage broker and your solicitor.
- Include a short paragraph about yourself, to convince the seller to choose your offer! Be sure to mention if you’re a first-time buyer (able to move quickly), or have a Mortgage in Principle.
- By law, estate agents have to tell the seller the details of every offer they receive.
- You should get a call from the agent within the next 24 hours, confirming if your offer was accepted or declined!
- If your offer was declined, decide if you want to increase it (and can afford to), or if it’s better to cut your losses.

Tips to make your offer stand out

- The most obvious way to win a bidding war is to offer the highest price. But, that’s not always possible – and you shouldn’t forget your budget or overstretch yourself in the heat of the moment.
- Our top tip is to have a Mortgage in Principle, as this shows you’ve done your research and can borrow enough to afford the property.
- It’s an old trick, but offering an unusual number can help your offer stand out – especially if there have been lots of others at a similar level. For example, offering £250,500 if the asking price is £250,000.
- Finally, being available for phone calls and emails when there are other offers on the table could make the difference between having your offer accepted, or the seller going with someone else. Try and build a rapport with the agent!
