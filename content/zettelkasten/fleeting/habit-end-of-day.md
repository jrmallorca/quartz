# End of day habit

## Reason

Ensure house is secured and organised.

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- You start to feel like sleeping.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Check all doors and windows are locked.
- Check contents in dish rack that are dry are stowed away.
- Check music isn't playing.
- Check downstairs is tidy, starting from kitchen to entry.
- Close off all downstairs lights and turn on upstairs lights.
- Check upstairs is tidy, starting from the office to the main bedroom.

## [[reward-in-terms-of-habits|Rewards]]

Sleep.
