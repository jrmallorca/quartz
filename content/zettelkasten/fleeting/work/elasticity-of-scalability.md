# Elasticity of scalability

The elasticity determines the difficulty involved in the [[scalability-of-systems]] through automatic scaling.

More elasticity means it's less difficult, or more automatic, to scale systems and vice versa.

## Flashcards

What does the **elasticity of scaling systems** determine? :: The difficulty or automaticity in scaling systems.

What does _MORE elasticity_ mean when **scaling systems**? :: Scaling systems is easier or more automatic.

What does _LESS elasticity_ mean when **scaling systems**? :: Scaling systems is harder or less automatic.
