# Power outages can cause a lack of availability

A power outage, even a quick power flicker, can cause computers to reboot and systems to restart. When that happens, the application will be unavailable until all systems are restored.

## Flashcards

What will happen to a _running application_ if the **host experiences power outages**? :: Application will be unavailable.

What will happen to _host computers and systems to an application_ if **power outages occur**? :: Computers will reboot and systems will restart.
