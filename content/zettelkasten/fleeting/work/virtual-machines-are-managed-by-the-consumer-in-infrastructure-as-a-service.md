# Virtual machines are managed by the consumer in Infrastructure as a Service

As [[infrastructure-as-a-service-gives-maximum-control-of-given-resources-versus-other-services]], the [[infrastructure-as-a-service-allocates-virtual-machines-as-a-resource]] and has to be fully managed by the consumer.

This means that the responsibility of installing and maintaining the operating system and other necessary services for an application falls on the consumer.

However, [[zettelkasten-for-work/zettelkasten/permanent/cloud-providers-monitor-the-health-of-their-computing-resources]] so the health of the resource will be managed by [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]].
