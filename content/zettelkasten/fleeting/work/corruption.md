# Corruption

Corruption occurs when individuals in positions of power abuse their authority for advantages, like these [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/examples-of-corruption]], and involves at least two parties: the abuser and the person who hopes to gain an advantage from the abuse. [[corruption-must-be-intentional-and-advantagous-to-involved-parties]] to be considered corruption.

## Flashcards

What is **corruption**? :: Abuse of authority from individuals in positions of power.

Why does _corruption_ **happen**? :: Advantages, but most likely profit.

For _corruption_, which **parties are involved**? :: Abusers and those who want to gain an advantage from the abuse.
