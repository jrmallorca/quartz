# Examples of encouraging audience participation in presentations

Rather than keeping a presentation as a monologue, [[encourage-participation-from-audience-in-presentations-to-regain-or-keep-attention]] to regain or keep their attention. This could be achieved by the following:

- Ask questions to audience.
- Encourage conversation between speaker and audience.
- Encourage conversation between audience members.
- Encourage an activity for audience to perform.
