# Hybrid cloud may experience application slowdowns due to geographical distances

As the [[hybrid-cloud-is-a-combination-of-public-and-private-clouds]], slowdowns may occur if the [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/public-cloud]] and [[private-cloud]] have a large geographical distance between them.
