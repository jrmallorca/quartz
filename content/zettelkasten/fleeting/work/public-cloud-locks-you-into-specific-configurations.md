# Public cloud locks you into specific configurations

Like the [[public-cloud-gives-up-control-of-infrastructure-for-the-company]], the [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/public-cloud]] can lock you into specific configurations that the [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]] have chosen.

For example, suppose a company has an application that needs a large amount of disk storage, but only needs a single CPU system to run it. In order to meet the disk space requirements, the [[zettelkasten/fleeting/work/structure/cloud-providers]] might require you to use a high-powered, multi-CPU VM, thereby increasing your costs unnecessarily.
