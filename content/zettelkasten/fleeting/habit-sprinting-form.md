# Habit - Sprinting form

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

Before or during a sprint.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Ensure the balls of the feet make contact with the ground first (forefoot strike).
2. Ensure that the heel kisses the ground with each step (makes brief contact).
