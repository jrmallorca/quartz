# Snowball effect for habits

The [[snowball-effect]] can be implemented for [[habit]]s as they can affect our behaviour after completion of a habit. By implementing the [[2-minute-rule-for-habit-formation]], we can complete habits within a few seconds while impacting our behaviour for minutes or hours afterwards.

A negative example of the [[snowball-effect]] for [[habit]]s is the [[abstinence-violation-effect-can-destroy-a-formed-habit]].
