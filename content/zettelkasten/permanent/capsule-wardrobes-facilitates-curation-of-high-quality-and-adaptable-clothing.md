# Capsule wardrobes facilitates curation of high quality and adaptable clothing

As [[capsule-wardrobe]] encourages having a minimal amount of clothes that are versatile, it makes it easier to curate the wardrobe to have high quality clothing. It ensures the process of acquiring clothes is slow and intentional, thereby making it easier to save enough money to acquire clothes in general while loving every garment in the wardrobe.

Each piece of clothing must be:

- Versatile or a statement
- Desired
- Practically usable with the rest of the wardrobe
