# Lifetime ISA contributes towards yearly ISA allowance limit

The [[lifetime-isa]] contributes towards the [[annual-isa-allowance-limit-is-20000]; If you had put in £3000 in your [[lifetime-isa]], the remaining amount you can put into other ISAs is £17,000.
