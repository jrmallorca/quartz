# The impression left after interpersonal interactions are more important than details of the interactions

In each interaction with a person, the resulting impression will be determined from the feelings evoked throughout the interaction rather than specific details of what happened in the interaction. These [[impressions-left-can-become-anchors-to-the-halo-effect]].
