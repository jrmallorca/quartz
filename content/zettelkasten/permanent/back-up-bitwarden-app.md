# Back up Bitwarden app

1. Go to **Bitwarden** app.
2. Ensure you're in the **correct** account.
    1. At the _top bar_, press the **icon with 2 initials**.
    2. Select the **personal** account.
    3. **Login**.
3. At the _bottom right corner_, press **Settings**.
4. Scroll down to the _Tools_ section and press **Export vault**. 
5. Press the **File format** field.
6. Select **.json (Encrypted)**.
7. Press **OK**.
8. Fill in the **Master password** field.
9. Press **Export vault**.
10. In the _confirmation dialog_, press **Export vault**.
11. In the _File Manager_, navigate to the **backups** folder.
12. Appropriately **name** the file.
13. Press **Save**.
14. Repeat for all accounts.
