# Habit - Scrape and fill interior surfaces before painting

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- After [[habit-remove-obstacles-before-painting-interior-walls]].
- Before [[habit-key-surfaces-before-painting]].

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

- Fill any holes or indentations in interior surfaces.
