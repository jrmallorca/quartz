# Stories can increase the perception of value of products

Stories can increase the value of your offering.

Might be effective when paired with the [[goal-dilution-effect]].

## Analogy

## Concrete example

In "The StorySelling Method" book, there is a reference of how telling a story used to increase the value of an offering:

- 100 items were originally purchased for \$129 in total.
- They sold for \$3,613.

That’s roughly a 2,700% increase in value, all from including a short fictional story.
