# Variable interval intermittent reinforcement produces superstitious behaviour

Superstitious behaviour arises when a series of rewards/consequences happen closely alongside independent behaviour. Therefore, [[variable-interval-intermittent-reinforcement]] produces [[superstitious-behaviour]].

For example, pigeons in B.F Skinner's operant boxes were being given food pellets in random intervals. Pigeons may then connect an action they did (e.g., lifting their foot or turning their head left) to receiving food. This would then reinforce [[superstitious-behaviour]] as they would notice a connection to performing an action with the **independent** event of receiving food.
