# When someone confides in you, assume they want comfort rather than solutions until proven otherwise

When someone confides in you, it's often unclear whether they want comfort or solutions. [[giving-solutions-while-a-person-is-stressed-feels-like-the-listener-is-not-listening]]. As we should [[only-advise-when-solicited]], assume by default that they want comfort rather than your advice. If they seek solutions, they will often tell you in the form of some question like "what should I do?".
