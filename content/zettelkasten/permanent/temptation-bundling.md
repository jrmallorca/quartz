# Temptation bundling

Temptation bundling is a way to apply [[premacks-principle]] to [[habit-stacking]]. This uses the concept of [[superstimulus]] to heighten the desire of a habit we need to do by combining it with a habit we want to do.

Temptation bundling uses the following formula:

- After <CURRENT_HABIT>, I will <HABIT_I_NEED>.
- After <HABIT_I_NEED>, I will <HABIT_I_WANT>.
