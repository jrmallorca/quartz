# Halo effect

The Halo effect is a sub-pattern of the [[anchoring-effect]], describing the phenomenon where "people tend to bias their decisions on 'anchors' based on prior information--in the most ludicrous cases, even if the information was entirely irrelevant". These anchors may be from good or bad impressions left through [[interpersonal-interaction]].

The reverse of this is the horn effect.
