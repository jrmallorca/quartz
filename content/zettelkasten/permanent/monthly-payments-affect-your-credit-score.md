# Monthly payments for insurance affect your credit score unlike yearly payments

For insurance, the monthly payment options will affect your credit score as the insurance company is "lending" you money to pay for a year's worth of insurance. Due to this, the insurance company would run a hard credit check as part of the application process.

This is unlike [[annual-payments-for-insurance-does-not-affect-your-credit-score-unlike-monthly-payments]].
