# How to fold a spa towel

1. Lay the towel flat on a flat surface.
2. Fold the longest side of the towel in half.
    - This step is optional if the towel is already close to the shape of a square.
3. Take 2 corners that oppose each other.
4. Fold one corner a third of the way to the opposing corner.
5. Fold the other corner a third of the way to its opposing corner. The shape should now look like a long hexagon.
6. Flip the towel over.
7. Roll the corner from one end to the other.
8. Tuck in the corner in the pocket created that's shaped like a V.
