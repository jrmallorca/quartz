# Decisive moments for habits

Many habits occur at decisive moments. Decisive moments are choices that can send you either to a productive or unproductive day.

By utilising [[snowball-effect-for-habits]], we [[make-the-response-easy]] to choose the productive option.

Examples of decisive moments:
- Deciding whether to exercise or not.
- Seeing dessert and either refusing or accepting it.
