# Back up with Proton Drive app

After completing all back ups:

1. Go to Proton Drive app.
2. Press the **backups** folder.
3. At the _top right corner_, press **+**.
4. Press **Upload a file**.
5. In the _File Manager_, navigate to the **backups** folder.
6. Select the file to back up.
7. Repeat until all files are backed up.
