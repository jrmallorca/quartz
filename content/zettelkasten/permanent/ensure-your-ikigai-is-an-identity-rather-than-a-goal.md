# Ensure your ikigai is an identity rather than a goal

In order to reap the benefits from the note [[ikigai-can-bring-satisfaction-happiness-and-meaning-to-life]], ensure your [[ikigai]] is an identity in [[identity-based-habits]] rather than [[outcome-based-habits]].

We can see many similarities with [[ikigai]] and [[identity-based-habits]] with their concepts and benefits:

- As [[goals-are-at-odds-with-long-term-progress]], it may make you "lose your purpose" once you achieve it.
- [[setting-goals-can-restrict-your-happiness-until-its-achievement]].
