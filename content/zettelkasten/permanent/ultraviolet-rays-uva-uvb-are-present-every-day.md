# Ultraviolet rays (UVA/UVB) are present every day

Ultraviolet rays from the sun, UVA/UVB, are present every day, and are especially concerning in the summer due to likely increased skin exposure from the heat. However, UVA/UVB does not disappear in the winter.

This is the reason why [[sunscreen-should-be-used-every-day]].
