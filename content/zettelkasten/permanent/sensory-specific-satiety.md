# Sensory-specific satiety

Sensory-specific satiety refers to the degradation of pleasure from the consumption of food within a single eating occasion.

Eventually our brain gets used to the [[orosensation]] within the food. Our sensors are sated.

This is the reason why the first 3 bites of a dish are the most enjoyable.
