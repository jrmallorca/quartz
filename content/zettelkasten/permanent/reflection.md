# Reflection

Reflection is the act of looking back on past experiences to understand and learn from it.

Through experiences, we are able to gather what happened, how it happened, why it happened, and how we could improve it.

## Concrete example

During each exercise, we film ourselves to check [[correct-form-in-exercise]]. After the exercise, we review the video to see how we can correct ourselves further.
