# Intensity of cognition is directly correlated to novelty of stimuli

When an activity is not yet a [[habit]], i.e., the first stage of [[reinforcement-learning-in-animals]], mental stimulation is heightened as it is exposed to novel information. As a [[habit]] starts to form for the activity, mental stimulation reduces. [[habit-formation-minimises-cognitive-load-usage-for-other-activities]].

Therefore, intensity of cognition is directly correlated to novelty of stimuli.
