# Feynman technique reveals what we do not know

The [[feynman-technique]] is an effective method as [[testing-is-an-effective-tool-for-learning]]. It reveals gaps in our knowledge and forces us to acknowledge what we don't know about a subject; it doesn't allow for [[illusion-of-mastery]].

As the [[feynman-technique-tests-understanding-of-a-topic-by-the-efficacy-of-explaining-to-yourself-and-to-others]], if a topic could not be explained with a simpler format then the topic has not been fully understood and it should be revised.
