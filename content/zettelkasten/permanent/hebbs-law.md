# Hebb's Law

"Neurons that fire together wire together."

[[long-term-potentiation]] is the principle behind Hebb's Law.

"When release of transmitter at a synapse is repeatedly correlated with firing of action potentials in the postsynaptic neuron, they become stronger." This might be the basis of [[classical-pavlovian-conditioning]].
