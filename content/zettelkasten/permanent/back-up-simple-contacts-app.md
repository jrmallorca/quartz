# Back up Simple Contacts app

1. Go to **Contacts** app.
2. At the _top right corner_, press the **ellipsis (three dots)** icon.
3. Press **Export contacts to a .vcf file**.
4. Press **OK**.
5. In the File Manager, navigate to the backups folder.
6. Appropriately **name** the file.
7. Press **Save**.
