# Order of painting interior of rooms

Ideally, the order of painting the interior of rooms are the following:

1. [[painting-trim]]
2. [[painting-ceilings]]
3. [[habit-painting-interior-surfaces]]

Trim should be painted first as we typically [[paint-trim-one-level-higher-in-terms-of-gloss]]; glossier paint is much harder to remove.

Next are the ceilings as we can repair any accidental paint towards the walls, if needed. If the ceiling and the walls are the same paint, then you do not need to be careful when cutting in the ceiling/wall.

Lastly are the walls itself.
