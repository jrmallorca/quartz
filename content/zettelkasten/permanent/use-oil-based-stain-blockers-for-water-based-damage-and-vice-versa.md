# Use oil-based stain blockers for water-based damage and vice versa

As [[oil-and-water-do-not-mix]],  use oil-based stain blockers for water-based damage and vice versa.

This would apply to cases such as water damage travelling from bathroom to its respective room downstairs.

An example product is [Zinsser Cover Stain](https://www.zinsseruk.com/product/cover-stain/).
