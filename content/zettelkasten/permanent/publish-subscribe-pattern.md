# Publish/subscribe pattern (Pub/Sub)

The Publish/Subscribe pattern (Pub/Sub) is a messaging pattern where a publisher will send a message to all consumers subscribed to a message bus.

Emails are an example where the pub/sub pattern is used; users subscribed to an email newsletter will be sent an email whenever a publisher sends.

## Analogy

An email newsletter where a publisher will generate a newsletter for everyone who has subscribed to them.
