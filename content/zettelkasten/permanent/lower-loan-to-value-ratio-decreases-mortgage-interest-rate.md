# Lower loan-to-value ratio decreases mortgage interest rate

As the [[loan-to-value-ratio]] is a lending risk for lenders, being able to lower that risk will lower mortgage interest rates.

This is also related to [[lower-loan-to-value-ratio-decreases-mortgage-interest-rate]].

## Flashcards

Why would a LOWER _loan-to-value (LTV) ratio_ DECREASE mortgage **interest rate**? :: You are seen as less of a risk to lenders.
