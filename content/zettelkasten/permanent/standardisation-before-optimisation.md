# Standardisation before optimisation

A particularly important trait in the [[organise-standardise-optimise]] process is that standardisation happens **before** optimisation. As stated in the note [[fear-of-failure-can-stagnate-progress]], we may confuse progress from the organise and the optimise steps. In reality, it is the standardise step, the act of the activity itself, that progresses us towards a desired result.

From the standardise step, we can see both our successes and failures to learn on how we could make ourselves better iteratively.
