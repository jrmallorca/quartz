# The best version of yourself requires continuous reflection

To take advantage of [[identity-based-habits]], there must be continuous reflection on the following:

- the identities to associate with,
- the habits associated with the identity,
- and the factors that form those identities.

A rough idea from earlier on that relates to this is to [[love-your-future-self-to-love-your-past-self]].
