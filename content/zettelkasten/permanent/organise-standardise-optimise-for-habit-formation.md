# Organise, standardise, optimise for habit formation

[[organise-standardise-optimise]] is important for habit formation as it will [[make-the-response-easy]].

For example, the habit of "exercise" can be broken down to many actions, most of which may depend on the person as well:

1. Show up to the exercise environment (e.g., gym or living room).
2. Perform an exercise regime.
3. Once finished, clean up exercise equipment and leave.

The [[gateway-habit]] can just be the first action, show up to the exercise environment.

Once the [[gateway-habit]] has been established, a standard has been set to always show up. This can [[make-the-response-easy]] as we [[design-your-environment-for-your-habits]] (i.e., primed your environment for exercise). The next thing to standardise would then be the performance of the actual exercises itself through the [[said-principle]].

Optimisation can be done later for any of the steps. For example, investing in a home gym can optimise the time it takes to reach the exercise environment. The [[correct-form-in-exercise]] can be achieved in time as [[the-most-effective-lessons-come-from-action-not-planning]]. Alternatively, the ultimate end goal of [[organise-standardise-optimise]] could also be once we can [[automate-habits]].
