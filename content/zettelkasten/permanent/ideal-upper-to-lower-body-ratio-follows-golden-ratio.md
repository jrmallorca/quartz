# Ideal upper to lower body ratio follows golden ratio

The ideal upper to lower body ratio follows the [[golden-ratio]]. It's more attractive to have the appearance of having more legs than torso.

Side note: one of Leonardo Da Vinci's, the Vitruvian Man, determines the ideal upper and lower body ratio as stated by Vitruvius is 3.75 : 6.25.
