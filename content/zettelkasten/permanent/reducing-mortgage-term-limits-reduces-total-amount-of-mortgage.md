# Reducing mortgage term limits reduces total amount of mortgage

[[overpaying-mortgage-reduces-terms]] which reduces the total amount of money required to pay off the mortgage.

This is achieved by reducing the power of [[compound-interest]] of the mortgage (i.e., amount of time the interest can build the mortgage).
