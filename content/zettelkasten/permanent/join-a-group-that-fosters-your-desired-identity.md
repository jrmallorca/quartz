# Join a group that fosters your desired identity

To further an [[identity-based-habits]], join a group that has the following:

1. Your desired behaviour is the norm.
2. (Optional) You have something in common with the group.

The reason this works is because of [[maslows-hierarchy-of-needs-3rd-layer]] and how [[humans-have-a-need-to-belong]].
