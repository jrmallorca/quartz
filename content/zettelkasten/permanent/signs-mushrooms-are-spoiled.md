# Signs mushrooms are spoiled

1. Mushrooms are slimy.
2. Mushrooms are wrinkly (and appears dehydrated).
3. Mushrooms are discoloured.
4. Mushrooms have a sour/fishy smell.
5. Mushrooms have a floppy feel.
