# Government contribution in Lifetime ISA is 25%

The government will contribute 25% of whatever you deposit into a [[lifetime-isa]] per year. Therefore, the maximum contribution given from the government per year is £1000 for depositing £4000 into the [[lifetime-isa]].

Keep in mind that [[withdrawals-from-the-lifetime-isa-incur-a-25-percent-penalty-if-not-used-for-its-designed-purposes]].
