# Long-term potentiation is the basis of classical (Pavlovian) conditioning

From the "Synaptic Signaling in Learning and Memory":

> When release of transmitter at a synapse is repeatedly correlated with firing of action potentials in the postsynaptic neuron, they become stronger.

[[long-term-potentiation]], as summarised by [[hebbs-law]], can be the cause of [[classical-pavlovian-conditioning]] from repeated association of certain neurons "firing together".
