# Humans have a need to belong

Humans have a need to belong to the tribe as seen in [[maslows-hierarchy-of-needs-3rd-layer]].

Historically, to not belong to the tribe is to be outcast and potentially killed. As said in [Atomic Habits](../../books/atomic-habits.epub):

> When changing your habits means challenging the tribe, change is unattractive. When changing your habits means fitting in with the tribe, change is very attractive.

There is tremendous pressure to comply with the group, even if it means being inferior.
