# Frequent and low-stakes testing reduces test anxiety through familiarity and spread consequences

Frequent and low-stakes testing reduces test anxiety among students by spreading the consequences over a larger sample: no single test is a make-or-break event.

The frequency will also familiarise students with the action of testing as it becomes a [[habit]].

## Concrete example

Imagine any activity that was once scary or unfamiliar. After repetition, [[habit-formation-naturally-optimises-behaviour]] and it becomes familiar and easier. This applies for frequent testing as well.

For example, [[spaced-repetition]].
