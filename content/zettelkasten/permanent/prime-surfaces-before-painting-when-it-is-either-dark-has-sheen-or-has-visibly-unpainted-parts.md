# Prime surfaces before painting when it is either: dark, has sheen, or has visibly unpainted parts

When a surface has any of the following, it is necessary to prime them after [[habit-key-surfaces-before-painting]]:

- Sheen.
- Parts where it is bare.
- Dark-coloured paint.
