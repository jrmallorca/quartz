# Cue (in terms of habits)

The 1st part of [[habits-are-made-of-cues-cravings-responses-and-rewards]], a cue is a trigger for a behaviour. It's the initial hint that predicts a [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]].

Including the cue, most of the process of [[habits-follow-maslows-hierarchy-of-needs]].

After the cue follows the [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]].
