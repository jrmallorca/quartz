# Self-control is a short-term strategy

As said in [Atomic Habits](../../books/atomic-habits.epub),

> Self-control is a short-term strategy, not a long-term one. You may be able to resist temptation once or twice, but it’s unlikely you can muster the willpower to override your desires every time.

Those who are perceived to be "disciplined" have structured their lives to not require constant tests of willpower or self-control. They either [[make-the-cue-obvious]] or [[make-the-cue-invisible]] when appropriate, or have lived in an environment where those 2 have been applied.

Just imagine controlling your diet when you're constantly surrounded with easily available and delicious snacks. Imagine a person controlling porn habits when it is a web search away and the dopamine receptors light up from the immense variety and immediate satisfaction.

This emphasises the importance to [[design-your-environment-for-your-habits]].
