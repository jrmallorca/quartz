# Drink water before brushing teeth

According to [this article](https://vitarellidental.com/is-it-safe-to-drink-water-before-you-brush-your-teeth-in-the-morning/), drinking water before brushing the teeth in the morning will wash the saliva and bacteria into our gut. This will kill the bacteria due to the high acidity presence.

[[drinking-water-is-beneficial]] and the benefits will be activated the earlier water is consumed during the day.

## Flashcards

When we _drink water before brushing the teeth_, what happens to the **bacteria** present in the mouth? :: It is moved to the gut and killed by acid.
