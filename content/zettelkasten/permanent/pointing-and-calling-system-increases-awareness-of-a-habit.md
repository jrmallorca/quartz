# Pointing-and-Calling system increases awareness of a habit

As [[habit-formation-minimises-cognitive-load-usage-for-other-activities]], an activity that becomes a [[habit]] also becomes automatic and can be overlooked.

As [[behaviour-change-requires-awareness-of-current-habits]], the [[pointing-and-calling-system]] can increase the awareness of a behaviour as there is verbal acknowledgement of each part of the [[habits-are-made-of-cues-cravings-responses-and-rewards]].

The weight of a bad consequence due to a habit becomes more real and significant than when it is performed automatically.
