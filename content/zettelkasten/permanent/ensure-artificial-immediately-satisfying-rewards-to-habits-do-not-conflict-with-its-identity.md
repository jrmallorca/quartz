# Ensure artificial immediately satisfying rewards to habits do not conflict with its identity

When we [[make-an-immediately-satisfying-reward-for-a-habit-without-one]], we have to ensure the [[habit]] doesn't conflict with any of the identities we have chosen.

While [[association-with-identity-does-not-require-perfection-for-success]], if the conflicting habits win against the harmonious habits the majority of the time, the association to the identity may be broken.

For example, do not create the association of ice cream after an exercise. It conflicts with the identity of being healthy.

Instead, the rewards should progress us towards our goals like [[rewards-from-gamification]].
