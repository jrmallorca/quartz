# Motivation for MPB determines efficacy of Premack's principle

For [[premacks-principle]] to be effective, motivation for the [[mpb-in-premacks-principle]] must be sufficient enough.

Consider the 1st example in [[premacks-principle-is-also-known-as-grandmas-rule]]:

- To have 1 hour of gaming ([[mpb-in-premacks-principle]]), you must complete your homework ([[lpb-in-premacks-principle]]).

For [[premacks-principle]] to take effect, there must be significant motivation for the [[mpb-in-premacks-principle]]. If gaming is not motivating enough, the performance of the [[lpb-in-premacks-principle]] may not occur.
