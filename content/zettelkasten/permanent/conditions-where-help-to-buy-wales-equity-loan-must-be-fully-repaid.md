# Conditions where Help to Buy - Wales equity loan must be fully repaid

[[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]] must be paid off when any of the following conditions are fulfilled:

- Repayment mortgage is fully paid.
- Property is being sold.
- The end of the loan term has been reached (25 years).
