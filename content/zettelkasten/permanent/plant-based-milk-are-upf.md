# Plant-based milk are UPF

Plant-based milk are likely [[ultra-processed-food-upf]] as they can include vegetable oils, added sweeteners, added thickners, etc.

Fortification of plant-based milk through the addition of vitamins can make it UPF, but it also helps it contain the same vitamins in cow's milk.
