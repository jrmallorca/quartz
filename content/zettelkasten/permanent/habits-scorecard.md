# Habits Scorecard

The "Habits Scorecard" is an exercise from [Atomic Habits](../../books/atomic-habits.epub) to facilitate [[behaviour-change-requires-awareness-of-current-habits]].

The scorecard is a 2-step process:

1. Create a list of your daily habits,
2. Score each habit's net outcome in the future as either:
    - Good (+)
    - Bad (-)
    - Neutral (=)

Here is an example:
- Wake up =
- Turn off alarm =
- Check my phone –
- Go to the bathroom =
- Weigh myself +
- Take a shower +
- Brush my teeth +
- Floss my teeth +
- Put on deodorant +
- Hang up towel to dry =
- Get dressed =
- Make a cup of tea +
