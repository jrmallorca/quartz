# Vinyl matt paint is unsuitable for high traffic areas

Vinyl matt is unsuitable for high-traffic areas as they aren't designed for these situations. While [[trade-paint-is-better-than-retail-paint]], it will not last long when subdued to high traffic.

For example, vinyl matt paint used in a bathroom will leave streaks with water condensation.

An alternative would be [[trade-paint-with-durable-in-the-name-is-suitable-for-high-traffic-areas]].
