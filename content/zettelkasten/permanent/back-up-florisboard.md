# Back up FlorisBoard

1. Go to **FlorisBoard** app.
2. Scroll down and press **Advanced**.
3. Press **Back up data**.
4. Press **Back up**.
5. In the _File Manager_, navigate to the **backups** folder.
6. Appropriately **name** the file.
7. Press **Save**.
