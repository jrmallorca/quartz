# Apply moisturiser immediately after washing yourself

After washing yourself is the best time to apply a [[moisturiser]] due to the following:

- The [[humectant]] can extract moisture from the currently humid environment.
- The [[occlusive]] would be able to trap generous amounts of moisture.
