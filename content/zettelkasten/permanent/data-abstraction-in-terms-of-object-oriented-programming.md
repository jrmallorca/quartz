# Data abstraction, in terms of object-oriented programming

Data abstraction refers to only exposing information that a consumer needs, hiding the internal implementations of the actual functionality.

For example, consider a person driving a car. The person knows that pressing the `accelerator()` will accelerate the car, while pressing the `brake()` will stop the car. However, they don't know exactly how pressing the `accelerator()` will increase the speed of the car.
