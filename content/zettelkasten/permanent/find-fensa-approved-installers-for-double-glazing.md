# Find FENSA-approved installers for double glazing

FENSA is a government-authorised scheme that monitors building regulation compliance for replacement windows and doors. FENSA-approved installers give a FENSA certifcate that proves an installation:

- complies with building regulations,
- is registered with the local council,
- and has a guarantee insurance.
