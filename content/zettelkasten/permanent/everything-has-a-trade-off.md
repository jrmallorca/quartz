# Everything has a trade-off

It is very rare to encounter direct improvements, whether that be a method or a tool. This is unlike in video games where concepts are more concrete; a steel sword is likely to be superior to a wooden sword.

For example, [[merino-wool|merino wool]]. It has many benefits, but we've encountered a major drawback to hand wash due to its fragility and current circumstances (I can't machine wash in my parents' home).

Even [[restarting-exercise-via-body-weight-fitness]], of which has very obvious benefits to almost every aspect of life. But the effort and time commitment required is enough to tempt people (and me) not to do it, [even if it's a matter of life and death](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2748983/).

When we encounter a concept that seemingly improves an aspect of our life, we sacrifice our current way of life. We actively choose to trade the advantages and disadvantages. The disadvantages could be mitigated/negated through another concept, but that would still be introducing a trade-off.
