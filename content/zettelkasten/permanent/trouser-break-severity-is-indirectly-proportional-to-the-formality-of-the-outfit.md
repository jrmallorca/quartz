# Trouser break severity is indirectly proportional to the formality of the outfit

Defining severity as how significant the [[trouser-break]] is, the severity of the [[trouser-break]] can, slightly, determine the formality of the outfit.

In this situation, formality is determined by the cleanliness or sleekness of the outfit.
