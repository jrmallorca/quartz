# Massed practice results in the worst information retention rate

In terms of the [[forgetting-curve]], using [[massed-practice]] is like constantly extending the 1st session of [[spaced-repetition]] which does not induce [[desirable-difficulty-in-learning]] and therefore [[embedding-new-learning-in-long-term-memory-requires-a-process-of-consolidation]] does not repeat.

This is observed when [[spaced-repetition-reduces-exponential-decay-of-retained-information]]. Due to this, [[massed-practice-gives-the-illusion-of-mastery]].
