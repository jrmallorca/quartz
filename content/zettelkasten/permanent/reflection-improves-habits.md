# Reflection improves habits

[[reflection]] enables long-term improvement of all [[habit]]s as it raises awareness of our strengths and weaknesses, and how to improve upon them.

[[reflection-is-more-effective-with-more-data]].

## Concrete example

Data from the habits, such as [[streak]]s, heart rate, weight, etc., can help with potential areas to improve upon.
