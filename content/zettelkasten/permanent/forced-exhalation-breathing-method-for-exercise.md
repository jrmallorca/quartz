# Forced exhalation breathing method for exercise

1. [[brace-the-core-when-exercising]]
2. Breathe in before the [[muscle-lengthening]] phase of the lift.
3. Hold breath during the [[muscle-lengthening]] phase of the lift.
4. Partially release breath during the [[muscle-contraction]] phase of the lift.
5. Breathe out after the [[muscle-contraction]] phase of the lift.

(Forced exhalation works a lot like when you did martial arts; do a forced "tssst" exhalation after each movement.)
