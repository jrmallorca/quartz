# Good habits provide and grow the base for success in life

[[atomic-habits-are-the-compound-interest-of-self-improvement]]. Each [[habit]], [[atomic-habits]] or not, improves your life by 1% towards a specific goal. It improves the fundamentals of life.

As [[habit-formation-minimises-cognitive-load-usage-for-other-activities]], there is more cognitive capacity to be used for other activities and information, which can involve activities that specify the [[importance-of-leaving-the-comfort-zone-for-cognition]].

If the reserved cognitive capacity is then directed towards the formation of more [[atomic-habits]], we can continue growing our base rate of success in life.
