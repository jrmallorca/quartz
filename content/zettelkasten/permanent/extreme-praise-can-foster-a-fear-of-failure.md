# Extreme praise can foster a fear of failure

Interpreted sincerely, [[extreme-praise]] can set extreme standards for the person to keep achieving to receive praise.

Instead of a fostering a growth mindset, it can breed a fear of failure as anything that doesn't guarantee success will be avoided.
