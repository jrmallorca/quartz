# Autocatalysis

The term "autocatalysis" refers to a chemical reaction where the product is a catalyst of itself.

A catalyst is a substance that speeds up a reaction without being consumed.

Catalysis is the process of increasing the rate of reaction through addition of a catalyst.

Therefore it is the cause of a (self-sustaining) feedback loop.
