# Importance of muscular balance

Muscular balance is important for mainly 2 reasons:

1. Joints where only one muscle of a [[antagonistic-muscle-pairs]] is exercised will function poorly and will be more likely to be injured.
2. Aesthetically, the body will look unnatural.

## Flashcards

When only one muscle of an _antagonistic muscle pair_ is exercised regularly, how will the **joint** function? :: Poorly.
