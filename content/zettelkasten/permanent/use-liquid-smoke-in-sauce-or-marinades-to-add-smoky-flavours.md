# Use liquid smoke in sauce or marinades to add smoky flavours

We can use [[liquid-smoke]] to [[impart-smoky-flavour-to-food-while-cooking-indoors]]. Th flavour of smoke is highly versatile and can be added ino a wide variety of sauces and marinades.

Due to its high concentration, only a few drops are required. No more than 1/3 of a teaspoon's worth should be used.
