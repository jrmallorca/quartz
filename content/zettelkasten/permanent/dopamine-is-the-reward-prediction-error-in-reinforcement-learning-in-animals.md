# Dopamine is the reward prediction error (RPE) in reinforcement learning in animals

Once a [[habit]] has been formed through [[reinforcement-learning-in-animals]], we can gradually see the [[dopamine]] spikes transition from initially being part of the [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]] to being part of the [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]].

However, when a [[positive-reward-prediction-error]] or [[negative-reward-prediction-error]] occurs, the appropriate [[dopamine]] spike will occur in the [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]] phase.

This clarifies that [[dopamine-is-related-to-motivation]] and operates as the [[reward-prediction-error-rpe]] in [[reinforcement-learning-in-animals]].
