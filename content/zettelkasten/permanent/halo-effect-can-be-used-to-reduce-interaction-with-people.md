# Halo effect (horn effect) can be used to reduce interaction with people

We could exploit the negative [[halo-effect]] (horn effect) when we absolutely don't want to interact with certain people. A bad impression will make people less receptive, more critical, will attribute you for failures, etc.

If we do not care about the opinions of certain people, then a bad impression from them does not matter.
