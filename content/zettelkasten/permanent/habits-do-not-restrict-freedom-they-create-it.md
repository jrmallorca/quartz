# Habits do not restrict freedom they create it

The following is a potential perspective on [[zettelkasten/permanent/habit]]s taken from the book [Atomic Habits](../../books/atomic-habits.epub):

"Will habits make my life dull? I don’t want to pigeon-hole myself into a lifestyle I don’t enjoy. Doesn’t so much routine take away the vibrancy and spontaneity of life?"

The question is equivalent to "Have I mastered everything that life has to offer me?"

As [[habit-formation-minimises-cognitive-load-usage-for-other-activities]] and [[good-habits-provide-the-base-for-success-in-life]] it will free the cognitive capacity to take opportunities for or from the "vibrancy and spontaneity of life".
