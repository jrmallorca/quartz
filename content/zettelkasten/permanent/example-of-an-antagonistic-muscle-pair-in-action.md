# Example of an antagonistic muscle pair in action

To see the [[antagonistic-muscle-pair-roles]] in action, we will use the bicep curl.

During the _first phase_ of the bicep curl, the BICEPS are the **agonist** while the TRICEPS are the **antagonists**.

![[antagonistic-muscle-pair-in-the-first-phase-of-bicep-curls.png]]

During the _last phase_ of the bicep curl, the TRICEPS are the **agonist** while the BICEPS are the **antagonists.

![[antagonistic-muscle-pair-in-the-last-phase-of-bicep-curls.png]]
