# Small plates for food portion control are less effective the hungrier a person is

The efficacy of how [[small-plates-can-help-with-food-portion-control]] has an indirect correlation with hunger.

The hungrier a person is, the less likely a person is to fall for the [[size-contrast-illusion]]. This only occurs in terms of food, not for other occurrences of the [[size-contrast-illusion]].
