# Design your environment for your habits

To [[make-the-cue-obvious]] or [[make-the-response-easy]], you have to design the spaces around you to serve you as [[behaviour-is-defined-by-our-relationships-to-objects-in-the-environment]]. Every object or stable [[zettelkasten/permanent/habit]] should have a place and purpose.

This is especially important in your own home where you have the most control of your space and spend the most time in.

For example, to ensure that the bedroom only has bed-related habits in there, remove any cues that may conflict with that. These may be the following:

- Phone.
- Documents.
- Any technology.

After all, [[self-control-is-a-short-term-strategy]].
