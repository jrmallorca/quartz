# Create visual progress indicators for habits

A way to [[make-an-immediately-satisfying-reward-for-a-habit-without-one]] is to create visual progress indicators.

Imagine a job where you make phone calls all day. You have a quota (personal or not) to make a 100 phone calls every day. An example of a visual progress indicator is the following:

- 2 jars, one with 100 marbles and the other is empty.
- For each phone call, transfer a marble from one jar to the other.
- Repeat until all the marbles have been transferred to the other jar.

Another example is a [[streak]].
