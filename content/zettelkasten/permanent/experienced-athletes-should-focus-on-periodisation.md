# Experienced athletes should focus on periodisation

More experienced athletes will gain more benefits from [[periodisation]] as there is a stronger relationship between muscle mass and force output at this stage.

There is likely a stronger relationship between muscle mass and force output as the athletes are reaching the higher potentials of force output for the current size of their muscles. This means that the [[newbie-gains]] have succeeded in getting them the necessary muscle size and quality to perform [[correct-form-in-exercise]].

Due to this, [[periodisation]] will be more beneficial than chasing the diminishing returns of **only** training either hypertrophy, strength, endurance, etc.
