# Premack's Principle is also known as "Grandma's rule"

[[premacks-principle]] is also known as "Grandma's rule" as it is often applied practically in real life.

Consider the following examples:

- To have 1 hour of gaming, you must complete your homework.
- To eat a piece of chocolate, you must eat all your vegetables first.
