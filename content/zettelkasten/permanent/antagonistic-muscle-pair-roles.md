# Antagonistic muscle pair roles

In [[antagonistic-muscle-pairs]], the **agonist** is the muscle that performs [[muscle-contraction]] to move the body part, while the **antagonist** performs [[muscle-lengthening]].

## Flashcards

In an _antagonistic muscle pair_, what is the role of the **agonist**? :: The muscle that contracts (or is in 'agony').
