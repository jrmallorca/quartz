# Push up

## Primary muscle targets

- Pectoralis major (chest)
- Triceps brachii (back of arm)
- Anterior deltoid (front shoulder)

## Variations

## Form

![push-up-form-video](../attachments/push-up-form-video.mkv)

- Engage the core to straighten the back.
- Forearm should be vertical at all times.

### Starting position

- Tuck the chin to align the neck with the spine.
- Arms should be perpendicular to the floor.
- Hands should be slightly more than shoulder width apart.
- Make sure middle fingers are pointing at 11 and 1 o'clock.

### During exercise

- Shoulder should move like a clock hand.
- Ankle should perform a rocking motion to keep body straight.
- Toes should be pointed to perform a rocking motion with the ankles.

### End position

- Chest should be touching the floor.
- Make sure the head does not touch the floor before the chest.
- From a bird's eye view, the body should look like an arrow.
- From a bird's eye view, the body should not look like a T shape.
