# Decorator centres offer knowledge with decorating

The staff in decorator centres are very knowledgeable with decorating and will be happy to assist you in your problems.

Their advice comes from experience as decorators themselves and does not try to sell you the most expensive products. They account for both cost and practicality in their advice.
