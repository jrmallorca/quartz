# Combine talents to become better in a field

The combination of talents can result in becoming better within a field. The end result is either [[specialisation]] and/or possibly the creation of a new field. Both results end up reduction the amount of competition that may be better or worse than someone as they differ ourselves.

As [[zettelkasten]] is a tool primarily used for exploration of topics and their relevance to others, it can be used to observe topics that can be combined to become better.

For example, the creator of the cartoon Dilbert acknowledged he had better art, funnier jokes, and more business background than most people. Combining all 3 allowed him to excel in having a cartoon few cartoonists would have a background in.

Find/create a field where the odds are stacked in our favour.
