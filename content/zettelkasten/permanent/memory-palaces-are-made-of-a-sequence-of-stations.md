# Memory palaces are made of a sequence of loci

Every [[memory-palace]] should contain major loci and minor loci to create a logical sequence to "place" information onto.

Loci just means "place".

Major loci are typically rooms or sections of a venue.

Minor loci are the contents or furniture of the rooms. These could be the following:

- shelves,
- washing machine,
- couch,
- plant,
- etc.
