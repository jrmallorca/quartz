# Setting goals can restrict your happiness until its achievement

"The implicit assumption behind any goal is this: 'Once I reach my goal, then I'll be happy.'"

A side effect of setting goals is restricting happiness only when the desired result has been achieved; You either succeed and be happy, or fail and disappoint.

However, [[focusing-on-systems-rather-than-goals-does-not-restrict-happiness]].
