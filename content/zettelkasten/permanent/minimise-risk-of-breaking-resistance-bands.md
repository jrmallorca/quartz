# Minimise risk of breaking resistance bands

Resistance bands have been shown to fail and snap. This can cause potentially fatal injuries.

The following [link shows how to minimise the chance of resistance bands snapping](https://www.bestresistancebands.net/tips-stop-resistance-bands-snapping/)

## Flashcards
