# Rewards from gamification

The rewards from gamification [[make-an-immediately-satisfying-reward-for-a-habit-without-one]] and [[ensure-artificial-immediately-satisfying-rewards-to-habits-do-not-conflict-with-its-identity]].

Therefore, rewards from exercise should not involve consuming junk food. Instead, it could be the consumption of a lavish protein-filled meal. Rewards from knowledge-based work should not involve the consumption of social media.

General rewards could be financial or beneficial to life:

- Saving into a pot for a financial goal.
  - Amount of money could directly correlate with level.
  - This could be for an item, service, or holiday.
- Having a day off.
- Hanging out with friends.
- Buying ingredients for a lavish meal (e.g., steak).

Some rewards may be unfeasible, depending on the person. A person may be unable to take advantage of financial rewards if they don't have the capital for it.

To really make it addicting, make an [[immediately-satisfying-reward-through-variable-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]].

For tasks that have you consume time and effort instead of money, the reward could be saving the amount of money unspent towards a financial goal. For example, walking instead of taking the bus can put the bus fare towards savings for a desired item instead.

Levelling up and main quests would guarantee a reward. Main quests would be goals similar to new year resolutions.
