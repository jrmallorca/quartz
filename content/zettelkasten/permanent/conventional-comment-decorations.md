# Conventional comment decorations

## Non-blocking

Should not prevent the subject under review from being accepted.

- Helpful for organizations that consider comments blocking by default.

## Blocking

Should prevent the subject under review from being accepted, until it is resolved.

- Helpful for organizations that consider comments non-blocking by default.

## If-trivial

Gives some freedom to the author that they should resolve the comment only if the changes ends up being trivial.
