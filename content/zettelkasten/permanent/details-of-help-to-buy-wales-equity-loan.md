# Details of Help to Buy - Wales equity loan

[[percentage-value-of-the-help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]]

For the whole duration of the loan:

- You must pay a monthly £1 management fee by Direct Debit.

For the first 5 years:

- Loan is interest free.

From the 6th year onward:

- You must pay a monthly interest fee, that starts from 1.75% of the equity loan, until the loan is fully repaid.
- [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan-interest-rate-increase-calculation]]
- Interest rate increases every April, the start of a new tax year.
