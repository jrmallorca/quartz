# Identity-based habits are a feedback loop for associated identities

Repetition of a habit reinforces the identity associated with the habit. I.e., the more you repeat a behaviour, the more you reinforce the identity associated with that behaviour. Even if it's small, remember that [[atomic-habits-are-the-compound-interest-of-self-improvement]].

[[identity-based-habits]] are then a feedback loop.
