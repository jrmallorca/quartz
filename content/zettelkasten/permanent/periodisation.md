# Periodisation

Periodisation is phased variations in training intensity and volume to achieve a long-term goal.

In other words, [[training-volume]] will be changed for every [[mesocycle]] required to achieve a [[macrocycle]].

This technique helps to [[focus-on-one-goal-at-a-time]].
