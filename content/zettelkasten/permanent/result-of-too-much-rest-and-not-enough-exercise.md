# Result of too much rest and not enough exercise

By resting too much and exercising too little, you will not be able to get the benefits of [[resting-after-exercise-repairs-damage-to-body-and-builds-muscle]] as there will not be enough accumulated damage to the body to repair.

This may be due to an insufficient demand being given to the body which, according to the [[said-principle]], will stagnate progression and keep the body in homeostasis.
