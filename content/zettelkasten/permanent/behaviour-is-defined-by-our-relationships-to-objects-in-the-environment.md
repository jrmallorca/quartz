# Behaviour is defined by our relationships to objects in the environment

Our relationship, especially memories and feeling, with the objects in the environment can determine the type of each [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]].

For example, a couch might be a trigger to read a book for an hour. For another, it may be the trigger to watch TV and eat ice cream after work.
