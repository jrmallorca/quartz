# Lower loan-to-value ratio increases eligibility for mortgage

As the [[loan-to-value-ratio]] is a lending risk for lenders, being able to lower that risk will increase eligibility for a mortgage.

This is also related to [[lower-loan-to-value-ratio-decreases-mortgage-interest-rate]].

## Flashcards

Why would a LOWER _loan-to-value (LTV) ratio_ INCREASE **eligibility** for a mortgage? :: You are seen as less of a risk to lenders.
