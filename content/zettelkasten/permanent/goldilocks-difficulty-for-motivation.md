# Goldilocks difficulty for motivation

From Atomic Habits, the Goldilocks rule states that:

> ...humans experience peak motivation when working on tasks that are right on the edge of their current abilities. Not too hard. Not too easy. Just right.

Imagine souls-like games and how they're difficult but eventually achievable.
