# Concrete examples can help with understanding abstract topics

Abstract topics are easier to understand when grounded by a concrete example. Consider the following:

1. Abstract idea: Addition
   - Concrete example: Adding the amount of apples in a basket. If I added 2 apples in the basket with 4 apples already in it, we get 6 apples.
2. Abstract idea: Fractions
   - Concrete example: Sliced pizza. Each slice of pizza is a fraction that makes up the whole pizza.
3. Abstract idea: Conduction
   - Concrete example: Imagine a cold day and wrapping your hands around a mug of hot chocolate. Conduction warms your cold hands by transferring heat from the mug to your hands.
4. Abstract idea: principle of angular momentum.
    - Concrete example: a figure skater's rotation speeds up as their arms draw closer to the chest.
5. Abstract idea: conduction
    - Concrete example: a cup of hot chocolate warming up your hands on a cold day.
