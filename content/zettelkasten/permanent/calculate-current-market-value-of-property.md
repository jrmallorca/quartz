# Calculate current market value of property

According to the GOV.WALES [buyers guide](https://www.gov.wales/help-buy-wales-buyers-guide) , a surveyor from the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) can estimate the value of the home, based on its condition and the current housing market.
