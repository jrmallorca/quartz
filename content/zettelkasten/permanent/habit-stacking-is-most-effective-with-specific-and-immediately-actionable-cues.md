# Habit stacking is most effective with specific and immediately actionable cues

With [[habit-stacking]], it is best to ensure that the completion of a [[habit]] used as a [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] is highly specific and immediately actionable. This increases the [[likelihood-of-response-depends-on-craving-severity-capability-and-resistance]] as it would reduce any resistance associated with the habit.

As an example, imagine wanting to stack the habit of exercise during lunch time. The following cues are example of what could be used:

1. After it is 12:00, I will exercise.
    - This cue is quite vague. You would still have to consider whether you want to exercise before or after lunch as it could conflict with the habit of eating lunch.
2. After I close my laptop at lunch time, I will exercise.
    - This cue is a lot more specific. Most likely, this implicitly decides that exercise should happen before lunch and is less likely to conflict with other habits.
