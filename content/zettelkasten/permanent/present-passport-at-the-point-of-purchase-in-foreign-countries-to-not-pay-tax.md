# Present passport at the point of purchase in foreign countries to not pay tax

Instead of [[claim-for-tax-rebate-at-airports]], some stores in some foreign countries could be presented the passport to remove the requirement to pay tax.

However, it is important to ensure that you're in a store that has the capability to do this.
