# Resistance tubes with handles

Resistance tubes with handles have the following properties:

- Tubular shape
    - The hollowness of the tube is inversely proportional to its resistance.
- Have handles.
- Not a continuous loop.
- Non-constant length.
    - The best length is 48 inches/122cm.
- Heavy duty (if they're good quality).
