# Poles should be used when painting buildings

Using a long pole will make painting and its preparation more efficient.

A (long) pole will provide the following benefits:

- Reduce the need of stepladders.
- Reduce strain by keeping your hands mostly below the heart.
- Increase coverage when scraping, cleaning, sanding, painting, etc.
