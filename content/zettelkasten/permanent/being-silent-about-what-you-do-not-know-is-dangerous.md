# Being silent about what you do not know is dangerous

> Better to remain silent and be thought a fool than to speak and to remove all doubt.

This is a dangerous quote to follow, especially in a cooperative environment. Only through speaking up about what you don't know that people will be able to help you; recognition of a problem is the first step to solving it after all.

The benefit of knowing outweighs the negative of being thought "a fool".
