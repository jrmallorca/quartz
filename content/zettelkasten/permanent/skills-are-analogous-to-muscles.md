# Skills are analogous to muscles

Skills are analogous to muscles as they can involve either [[said-principle]] and/or [[neuroplasticity]].

A more physically involved skill (e.g., [[push-up]]) may rely more on [[said-principle]] by using [[progressive-overload]] to build the required muscle size and strength to perform the skill. However, it may also require [[neuroplasticity]] through [[long-term-potentiation]] to ensure [[correct-form-in-exercise]].

A more mentally involved skill (e.g., [[charisma]] or [[story-telling]] or piloting a plane) may rely more on [[neuroplasticity]] to develop the [[mental-model]] required to use and adapt it for the situation they're experiencing in.

Both physical and mental skills have the following possibilities:

- Become a [[zettelkasten/permanent/habit]] as it is trained through the [[organise-standardise-optimise]] process.
- Deteriorate without use (i.e., [[quality-of-performance-can-plateau-or-decrease-without-nurture]]).
- Become overused and fail (e.g., [[self-control-is-a-short-term-strategy]])
