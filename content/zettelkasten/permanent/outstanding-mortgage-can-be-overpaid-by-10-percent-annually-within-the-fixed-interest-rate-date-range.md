# Outstanding mortgage can be overpaid by 10% annually within the fixed interest rate date range

Typically, the outstanding amount of the mortgage can be overpaid by 10% annually while within the fixed interest rate date range.

For example, for a mortgage of £100,000, the maximum amount that can be paid without getting an [[mortgage-early-repayment-charge]] is £10,000 in the 1st year. In the 2nd year, it would then be whatever 10% of the remaining principal would be.
