# Charisma does not require extroversion, physical attraction, or a change in personality

As [[charisma-is-determined-by-perceived-presence-warmth-and-power]], [[charisma]] does not require extroversion, physical attraction, or a change in personality. Even better, [[charisma-can-make-you-attractive]].

While extroversion and physical attraction may be helpful in acquiring charisma, there are many real-life people who radiate charisma without having either or any of them:

- Winston Churchill (Attraction),
- Pete Davidson (Attraction),
- Johnny Depp (Extroversion),
- etc.
