# Do not use steam mop on glue-down LVT

Don't use steam mop on glue-down LVT. Using steam will reactivate the glue and ruin the flooring.

[[clean-glue-down-lvt-with-warm-or-hot-water]]
Warm or hot water is fine, just not steaming.
