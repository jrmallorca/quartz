# How to think of an identity to associate with

An identity can help with forming [[identity-based-habits]] for the results we want.

To think of an identity we wish to have, use the following steps:

1. Think of any [[outcome-based-habits]] that we wish to have.
2. Think of the types of identities that would associate with that habit and consistently achieve it.
3. Prove that we associate with that identity as [[good-habits-are-feedback-loops-for-achieving-desired-results-reinforcing-identity-and-improving-self-esteem]].
