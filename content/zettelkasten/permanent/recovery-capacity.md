# Recovery capacity

Recovery capacity is mainly determined by the following variables:

- [[training-age-and-work-capacity-determines-efficiency-to-perform-exercise]].
- Stress levels.
- Diet quality.
- Sleep quality.
- Hydration levels.

Apart from [[training-age]] and [[work-capacity-for-exercise]], other variables are more modifiable in a shorter amount of time. As [[recovery-capacity-is-proportional-to-capacity-of-training-volume]], optimisation of these variables is important.
