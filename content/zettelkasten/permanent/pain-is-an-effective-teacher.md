# Pain is an effective teacher

As phrased in Atomic Habits:

> If a failure is \[immediately\] painful, it gets fixed. If a failure is relatively \[immediately\] painless, it gets ignored.

It's also worth noting that painful failure may be avoided rather than fixed. [[fear-of-failure-can-stagnate-progress]].

It is [[reinforcement-learning-in-animals]] but with a focus on [[negative-reward-prediction-error]].
