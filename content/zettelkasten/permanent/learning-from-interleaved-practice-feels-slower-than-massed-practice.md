# Learning from interleaved practice feels slower than massed practice

The learning from [[interleaved-practice]] feels slower than learning from [[massed-practice]]. However, [[massed-practice-is-time-consuming]] in the overall time it takes to understand and remember the material deeply.

This is because [[interleaved-practice-creates-desirable-difficulties-for-learning-via-spacing-of-topics]].

Meanwhile, [[massed-practice-gives-the-illusion-of-mastery]].
