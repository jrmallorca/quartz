# Potentially use Cash ISAs, instead of savings accounts

As [[multiple-isas-of-the-same-type-can-be-opened-and-deposited-into-annually]], it might be worth using Cash ISAs as an alternative savings accounts. Consider the following:

- [[money-earned-within-isa-are-tax-exempt]] which should also be taken account for if you're nearing the maximum [[personal-savings-allowance]].
- The Stocks and Shares ISA (S&S ISA) cannot be feasibly allocated the full £20k.
- High interest rates which can push you near the maximum [[personal-savings-allowance]].

Previously, this idea could not be considered as you were restricted to only adding to 1 ISA annually, unless the other ISA was a Lifetime ISA (LISA).
