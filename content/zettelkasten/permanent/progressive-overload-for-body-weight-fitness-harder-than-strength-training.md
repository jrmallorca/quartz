# Progressive overload for body-weight fitness harder than strength training

According to [day 6 of the body-weight fitness primer](https://libreddit.kavin.rocks/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/krt8dz/bwf_primer_buildup_community_event_day_6/), [[progressive-overload]] for body-weight fitness is not as straight-forward as using freely manipulable weights.

Due to this, different [[correct-technique-in-exercise]] and exercises must be used to change the leverage and the load the targeted muscles are under. This causes [[progressive-overload-for-body-weight-exercises-are-more-intense-than-weights]], and as such [[body-weight-exercises-are-vulnerable-to-the-law-of-diminishing-returns]]
