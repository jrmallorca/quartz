# Elevate outfits by introducing novelty

Getting attraction from your clothes, positively or negatively, stems from the introduction of novelty relative to the environment.

Let's think of the typical clothes worn in a casual setting:

- White fitted t-shirt and blue fitted jeans
- Black tracksuits
- Black puffer coats.

In this situation, a common encounter, it's easy to elevate the typical outfit by introducing novelty such as:

- [[accessories-elevate-an-outfit]]
- Dressing a bit more formally/informally.
- Changing the typical colour used, [[colour-season]].
- Presenting a garment differently
    - [[french-tuck]]
    - [[pullovers-can-be-used-as-an-accessory]]
- [[layering-clothes]]
- Using natural materials with different textures
    - [[merino-wool]]
    - Linen
    - Seersucker cotton
    - Leather
- [[silhouette-in-clothing]]
