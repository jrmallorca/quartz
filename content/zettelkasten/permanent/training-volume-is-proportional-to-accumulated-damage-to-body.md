# Training volume is proportional to accumulated damage to body

The more [[training-volume]] is in an exercise, the more damage is accumulated to the body. This results in the following:

- [[accumulated-damage-to-body-is-proportional-to-capacity-for-muscular-growth]]
- [[accumulated-damage-to-body-is-proportional-to-time-required-for-recovery]]
