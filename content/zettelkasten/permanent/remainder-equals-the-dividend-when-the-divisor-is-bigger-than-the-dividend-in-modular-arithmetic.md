# Remainder equals the dividend when the divisor is bigger than the dividend in modular arithmetic

An alternative equation to [[division]] is:

$$
\begin{align}
a &= qm + r \\
\\
where \\
\\
a &= \text{Dividend} \\
q &= \text{Quotient} \\
m &= \text{Divisor} \\
r &= \text{Remainder} \\
0 &<= r < m \\
\end{align}
$$

Therefore, according to the equation, the remainder when 1 is divided by 2 is 1, since $1 = (0)(2) + 1$.
