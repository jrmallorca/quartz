# Bohr effect

The Bohr effect describes the following phenomenon:

> when the carbon dioxide levels in the blood increase or the blood becomes more acidic, haemoglobin tends to let go of its oxygen molecules. To put it simply, it’s like a signal for haemoglobin to release oxygen.
