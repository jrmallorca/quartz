# Reuse white spirit

After using white spirit to [[clean-oil-based-paint-on-brushes-with-white-spirit]], attempt to avoid disposing it by reusing it instead. White spirit is still effective for removing paint from applicators after usage.

It's recommended to reuse white spirits as disposing it is a hassle.
