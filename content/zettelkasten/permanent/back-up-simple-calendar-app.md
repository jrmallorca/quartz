# Back up Simple Calendar app

1. Go to **Calendar** app.
2. At the _top right corner_, press **Export events to an .ics file**.
3. Tick checkbox **Export past events too**.
4. Press **OK**.
5. In the _File Manager_, navigate to the **backups** folder.
6. Appropriately **name** the file.
7. Press **Save**.
