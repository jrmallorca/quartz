# Do not base decisions on the best agreement in principle offered

According to [this article](https://www.which.co.uk/money/mortgages-and-property/mortgages/getting-a-mortgage/mortgage-agreements-in-principle-aips-aekVS6J9x06b), don't base the decision on WHO to get the [[agreement-in-principle]] as they may be different at the time of purchasing a property.

## Flashcards

Why should you NOT **base your decision** on who offers the best _agreement in principle_? :: The actual amount lent may be different at the time of purchasing the property.
