# Humans tend to imitate habits of the close, the many, and the powerful

As another way to [[design-your-environment-for-your-habits]], humans tend to imitate 3 groups:

- The close
    - This refers to family, friends, etc.
- The many
    - This refers to society in general, or a mass group of people not necessarily known to the person.
    - For example, reading the reviews of a restaurant or product and deciding whether to pursue it from the reviews.
- The powerful
    - This refers to people who stand out among the crowd. Those who are admired, respected, powerful, successful, etc.
    - [[humans-tend-to-look-for-ways-to-stand-out-to-succeed]]
