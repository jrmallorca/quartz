# Fear of failure can stagnate progress

When we're planning to explore outside our comfort zone, we can delay failure by constantly being stuck in the theoretical: planning, strategising, and learning; it is the first part of [[organise-standardise-optimise]]. Ultimately, it is not progress. We can stay busy finding out the optimal, but [[the-most-effective-lessons-come-from-action-not-planning]]

A more potent fear of failure can even stifle us from exploring outside our [[comfort-zone]] and [[quality-of-performance-can-plateau-or-decrease-without-nurture]]. There are a myriad of reasons to explore outside the comfort zone, including the [[importance-of-leaving-the-comfort-zone-for-cognition]].

Being afraid of failure ultimately stagnates us from progression of anything. Remember that [[failure-is-a-lesson-if-not-fatal]].
