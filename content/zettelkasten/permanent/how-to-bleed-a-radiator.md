# How to bleed a radiator

To [[bleed-radiator]]:

1. Turn off heating system. [[do-not-bleed-radiators-when-they-are-hot]].
2. Locate the bleed valve on the radiator.
    - These are usually at the top sides.
3. Use a bleed key to **slowly** release the air from the radiator by turning it anti-clockwise.
    - Use a towel or container underneath where you're bleeding to catch any stray water.
4. As soon as water starts dripping down from the release, close the bleed valve by turning it clockwise.
    - This means that all trapped air has been released.
5. Potentially re-pressurise the boiler.
    - Bleeding radiators may reduce the pressure of the heating system.
