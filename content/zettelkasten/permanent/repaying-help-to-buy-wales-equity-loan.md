# Repaying Help to Buy - Wales equity loan

The following information has been gathered from GOV.WALES [buyers guide](https://www.gov.wales/help-buy-wales-buyers-guide) and the [repaying the shared equity loan](https://www.gov.wales/help-buy-wales/repaying-shared-equity-loan) page:

- The loan term is 25 years.
- [[staircasing-help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]]
- [[conditions-where-help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan-must-be-fully-repaid]]
- To repay the loan, you must [[calculate-current-market-value-of-property]] and get a Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS] valuation report.
