# Habits are made of cues, cravings, responses, and rewards

The process of forming a habit requires the following 4:

1. [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]]
2. [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]]
2. [[response-in-terms-of-habits]]
4. [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]]

These steps are the optimised version of the [[feedback-loop-of-all-human-behaviour]].

The cue is about noticing the reward. The craving is about wanting the reward. The response is about obtaining the reward.

If any of these steps are insufficient, e.g., [[likelihood-of-response-depends-on-craving-severity-capability-and-resistance]], then a habit will not be formed.
