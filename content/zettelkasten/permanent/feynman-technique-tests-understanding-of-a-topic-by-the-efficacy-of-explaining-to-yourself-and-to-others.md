# Feynman technique tests understanding of a topic by the efficacy of explaining to yourself and to others

The [[feynman-technique]] involves explaining a topic to yourself and/or to others to test one's understanding of a topic. Through this active learning process, the [[feynman-technique-reveals-what-we-do-not-know]].

If an explanation of a topic could not have its complexity reduced enough such that the recipient understands it, then the topic has not yet been fully understood and there are knowledge gaps to be filled in first.

The opposite also holds true where if an explanation of a topic can be simplified and tailored to the target audience, then understanding of the topic has been mastered.
