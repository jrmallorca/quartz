# Highly addictive habits are dopamine-driven feedback loops

Each highly-addictive [[habit]] is a concentrated [[dopamine]]-driven feedback loop, i.e., [[reinforcement-learning-in-animals]] with high concentrations of dopamine. [[concentration-of-dopamine-release-is-directly-correlated-to-craving-potency]] and [[habit-rewards-teach-worthwhile-actions-for-the-future]]. If the [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]] is a significant dopamine spike, then it will be highly addictive.

This is why [[bad-habits-are-engineered-to-be-superstimuli-from-corporations]].

Even basic habitual behaviours such as eating food, drinking water, having sex, and interacting socially can provide high dopamine spikes.
