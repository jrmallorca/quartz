# Effective learning skills are counterintuitive

The most effective strategies with learning are often counterintuitive: [[interleaved-practice]], [[varied-practice]], [[elaboration]], etc.; they will not feel as if you're learning what you're practising.

We can see [[interleaved-practice-creates-desirable-difficulties-for-learning-via-spacing-of-topics]] which removes the [[illusion-of-mastery]], unlike when [[massed-practice-gives-the-illusion-of-mastery]].
