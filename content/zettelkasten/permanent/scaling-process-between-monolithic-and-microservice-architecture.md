# Scaling process between monolithic and microservice architecture

When we [[scale-systems-in-the-direction-of-demand]] in a [[monolithic-architecture]], waste in computing resources may be produced as not all features within the codebase may require scaling.

In a [[microservice-architecture]], individual services that require the additional power can be scaled rather than every one.
