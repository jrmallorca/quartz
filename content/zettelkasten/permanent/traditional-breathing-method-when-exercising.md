# Traditional breathing method when exercising

According to [this article](https://www.self.com/story/how-to-breathe-during-a-workout), the following breathing method should be implemented:

- Breathe in during the [[muscle-lengthening]] phase of the lift.
- Breathe out during the [[muscle-contraction]] phase of the lift.
