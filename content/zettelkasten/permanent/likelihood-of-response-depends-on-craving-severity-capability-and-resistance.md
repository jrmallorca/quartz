# Likelihood of response depends on craving severity, capability, and resistance

Whether a [[response-in-terms-of-habits]] occurs depends on the following:

- Severity of the [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]].
- Capability of performing the [[habit]].
- Resistance associated with the [[habit]].
