# Reward, in terms of habits

The final part of [[habits-are-made-of-cues-cravings-responses-and-rewards]], the reward is the end goal of the [[habit]].

The reward is more about giving contentment and relief from a [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]]. This is an important distinction as the [[habit]] is not always necessarily beneficial to us; e.g., stress relief from smoking. Remember [[maslows-hierarchy-of-needs]].
