# Giving solutions while a person is stressed feels like the listener is not listening

When a person is stressed and is ranting about their problem, we don't know if they want comfort or solutions. If they want comfort, then giving solutions will make it seem like you're not listening to them rant; therefore, you're not listening and giving appropriate responses.
