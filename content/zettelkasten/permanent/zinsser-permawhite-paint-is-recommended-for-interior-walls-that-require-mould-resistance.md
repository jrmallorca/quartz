# Zinsser permawhite paint is recommended for interior walls that require mould resistance

[Zinsser Permawhite paint](https://www.zinsseruk.com/product/permawhite-interior/) is a recommended product for painting interior walls that require mould resistance, such as bathrooms or basements.

This is because the paint is:

- Self-priming
- Durable
- Scrubbable

However, opacity for its base white version is quite bad and may require 3 coats when killing dark colours.

This is typically used as an underlayer, rather than the topcoat of a wall.
