# Dopamine is not the pleasure molecule

Contrary to popular belief, [[dopamine]] is not the molecule that stimulates "pleasure". The reason for this is because older research showed dopamine neurons activating in the context of [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]]. But recent research, as of 2024-05-26, shows that [[dopamine-is-related-to-motivation]].
