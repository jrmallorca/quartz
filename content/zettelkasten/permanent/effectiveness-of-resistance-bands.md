# Effectiveness of resistance bands

Resistance bands are effective due to the following reasons:
- [[achieving-muscle-hypertrophy]]
- [[achieving-muscle-strength]]
- They can be stacked together to simulate heavier weights. This links with [[minimise-risk-of-breaking-resistance-bands]] and [[progressive-overload]].
- They are adaptable; they complement many, if not all, exercises.
