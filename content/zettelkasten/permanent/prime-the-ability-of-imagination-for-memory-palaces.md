# Prime the ability of imagination for memory palaces

[[memory-palace]]s require a degree of imagination to be utilised effectively. To do so, train imagination through the following exercise:

- Imagine a table with a candle in one corner and an apple in the other corner; Levitate them and try to switch them to different corners; As you move them, change their motion (e.g., zigzag or accelerate), colour, and size.
- Repeat the above exercise in different memory palaces, perhaps with different objects.
