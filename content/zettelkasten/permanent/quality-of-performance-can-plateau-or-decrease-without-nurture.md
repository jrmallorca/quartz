# Quality of performance can plateau or decrease without nurture

There are many who are perceived to be gifted early in life but cannot sustain the same performance later on. For example, people who did not need to study much in school suddenly suffering during university.

[[genetics-do-not-determine-destiny]]. For both the gifted and non-gifted, a plateau or decrease in the quality of performance in the same activity can occur if the [[habit]] to nurture them is non-existent. We can see this in [[focus-on-systems-not-goals]] where as long as the system of the habit is there, results will be achieved whether they were a goal or not. [[maintenance-of-results-requires-maintenance-of-systems]].

For both types of people, [[fear-of-failure-can-stagnate-progress]] as it ultimately stagnates us.
