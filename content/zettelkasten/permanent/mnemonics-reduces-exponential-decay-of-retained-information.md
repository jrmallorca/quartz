# Mnemonics reduces exponential decay of retained information

The format of which we remember our information can affect the severity of exponential decay of the [[forgetting-curve]]. There is an indirect correlation between how interested we are in the information we learn and the exponential decay of information.

The use of [[elaborative-encoding]] to make the information more interesting, while storing it in a [[memory-palace]] to take advantage of our brain's spatial mapping, will make information less likely to be forgotten.
