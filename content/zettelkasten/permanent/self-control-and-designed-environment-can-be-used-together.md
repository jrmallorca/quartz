# Self control and designed environment can be used together

When it comes to temptations, [[self-control-is-a-short-term-strategy]] as it is a tactic that **will** eventually fail. To [[design-your-environment-for-your-habits]] is to eliminate the bad [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] in the first place to not be constantly tested.

However, self control is useful for unprecedented temptation. For situations where you spend time in an environment you cannot design, you may be presented with the only option of controlling yourself.

Ultimately, there should be a practice of both self control and a designed environment. Self control is a skill and [[skills-are-analogous-to-muscles]]. It can be trained. It can also be overused and eventually fail. Therefore, a combination of both results in self control not being exhausted in environments that we can control and is less likely to fail in situations where we are tested.

## Analogy

Think of the [[biophilia-hypothesis]]. The fascination of nature uses our involuntary attention, letting our voluntary attention get some rest and recover. Like this, self control can be recovered in a designed environment and used in environments with temptations we cannot eliminate.

## Concrete example

For example, the home doesn't contain any snacks which heavily minimises the temptation to snack as it involves going out to the shop, purchasing it, then walking back.

However, the office is an environment we are limited in our authorisation to design and is present with snacks. Having the ability to control one's self should prove alright in this environment, provided one has practised self control and has not exhausted it elsewhere.
