# MPB in Premack's principle

The more probable behaviour (MPB) in [[premacks-principle]] is a behaviour more likely to be exhibited, usually due to having more motivation or innate entertainment from its performance.

It's usually a desired activity that is likely already a [[habit]].

MPB's could be the following:

- Gaming
- Eating dessert
- Sports
