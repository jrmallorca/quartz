# Progressive overload for body-weight exercises are more intense than weights

[[progressive-overload-for-body-weight-fitness-harder-than-strength-training]] as body-weight fitness is not as freely manipulable as weights.

Instead of small progressions of ~2-5kg with weights, progressing from one [[correct-technique-in-exercise]], or level of exercise to another, can feel like a sudden ~10kg change.

To alleviate this, [[progressive-overload-for-body-weight-fitness]] has a guide on how to not feel the sudden intensity.
