# Withdrawing a maximum of £4000 from a Lifetime ISA will not result in losing money

At the time of writing this, the [[maximum-amount-to-put-into-lifetime-isa-yearly-is-4000]].

While [[withdrawals-from-the-lifetime-isa-incur-a-25-percent-penalty-if-not-used-for-its-designed-purposes]], we are allowed to withdraw some of the money and not everything.

If we were to withdraw everything in the [[lifetime-isa]], and the value is more than £4000, then we would be losing both the [[government-contribution-in-lifetime-isa-is-25-percent]] and some of our own money.

However, if we keep withdrawing in instalments of a maximum of £4000, then all we should lose is the 25% government bonus.
