# Get an agreement in principle before putting an offer on a property

Start [[acquiring-an-agreement-in-principle]] before putting an offer for a property. Estate agents will want to ensure you are able to get a mortgage on a property before an offer.

## Related content

- [What is a mortgage in principle? - Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/mortgages-and-property/mortgages/getting-a-mortgage/mortgage-agreements-in-principle-aips-aekVS6J9x06b)

## Flashcards

When should you **get** an _agreement in principle_? :: After finding a mortgage advisor and started looking at properties but before putting an offer on a property.^1679697184528

Why should _an agreement in principle be acquired_ **before putting an offer on a property**? :: Estate agents want certainty that a person can get a mortgage on the property.^1741085378841
