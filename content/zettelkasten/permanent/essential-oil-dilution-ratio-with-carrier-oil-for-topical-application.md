# Essential oil dilution ratio with carrier oil for topical application

The concentration of the essential oils (e.g., rosemary oil, tea tree oil, etc.) for topical application varies, depending on where you apply it topically:

- For the face and scalp, it should be 1%-2%.
- For the body, it should be 2%-5%.

This keeps its efficacy without suffering the effects of applying its pure, concentrated version.
