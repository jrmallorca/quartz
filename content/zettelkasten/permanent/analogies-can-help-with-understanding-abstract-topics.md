# Analogies can help with understanding abstract topics

Abstract topics are easier to understand when compared against a concept already known through an [[analogy]]. It is more effective when grounded in reality, as demonstrated with [[concrete-examples-can-help-with-understanding-abstract-topics]].

Be careful as [[analogies-highlight-similarities-to-a-concept-and-eventually-break-down-the-further-it-is-used]]
