# Training volume

Training volume is defined as the following:

$$
\begin{align}
V &= \text{Volume} \\
S &= \text{Number of sets} \\
R &= \text{Number of reps} \\
L &= \text{Total load of exercise} \\
\\
V &= S \times R \times L
\end{align}
$$

This factor is important in exercise as [[training-volume-is-proportional-to-accumulated-damage-to-body]].
