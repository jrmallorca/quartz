# Implementation intentions are applications of classical/Pavlovian conditioning

An [[implementation-intention]] is an application of [[classical-pavlovian-conditioning]] where, given an effective cue and after an unspecified amount of repetitions, the [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] will eventually stimulate a [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]].
