# Accountability partner

Accountability partners are people that monitor your commitments and check if they're being achieved.

Accountability partners can [[make-the-reward-unsatisfying]] through the use of [[pain-is-an-effective-teacher]].

[[humans-have-a-need-to-belong]] and accountability partners make it painfully aware that someone is watching; Accountability partners induce motivation via social conformity as we care about the opinions from the accountability partner.
