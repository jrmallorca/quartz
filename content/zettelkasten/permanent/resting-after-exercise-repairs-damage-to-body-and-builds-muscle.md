# Resting after exercise repairs damage to body and builds muscle

According to [day 7 of the body-weight fitness primer](https://libreddit.kavin.rocks/r/bodyweightfitness/comments/ksiox7/bwf_primer_buildup_community_event_day_7/) and [this article](https://longevity.technology/lifestyle/what-happens-to-your-muscles-during-exercise-and-recovery/), satellite cells repair microscopic tears caused by exercise during recovery. The cells replicate, mature and fuse to damaged muscle fibres, forming a new muscle protein strand that increases the size and strength of the muscle to cope with increased demand. This is the [[said-principle]] in action.
