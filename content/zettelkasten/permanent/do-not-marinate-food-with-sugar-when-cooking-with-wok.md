# Do not marinate food with sugar when cooking with wok

Food that has been marinated with sugar will burn with the intensity of wok cooking. Instead, use the marinade as a sauce when the food is nearly done cooking instead.

As an alternative, we can [[velvet-meat]] before frying it and lathering it with sauce.

## Concrete example

Whenever we cook [[recipe-spicy-pork-bulgogi]] on the wok, we have to scrape off the burnt sugar which is really annoying. The burnt sugar then gets on our food which doesn't have good taste, texture, and health as it is carcinogenic.

Other methods of cooking might be better for food marinated with sugar (e.g., [[recipe-steam-air-fry-char-siu-pork]]).
