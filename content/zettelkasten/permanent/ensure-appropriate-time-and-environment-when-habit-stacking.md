# Ensure appropriate time and environment when habit stacking

[[habit-stacking]] needs both an appropriate [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] and environment to reduce any resistance associated with the habit.

For example, don't stack meditation when mornings are chaotic and kids are running around.
