# Good habits are easy to break in the beginning as results are slow to gain, intangible, and/or not immediately satisfying

Good habits are easy to break in the beginning because the results are either slow to gain, intangible, and/or not immediately satisfying. [[atomic-habits-are-the-compound-interest-of-self-improvement]], so it is expected that we only improve 1% at a time. Results are never instant.

For example, running only for a few weeks may only give you 2 more minutes of endurance. This miniscule gain may discourage the maintenance of the habit.

However, we can mitigate this with [[make-the-reward-satisfying]].
