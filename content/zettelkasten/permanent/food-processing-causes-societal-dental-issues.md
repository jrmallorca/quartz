# Food processing causes societal dental issues

The processing of food has caused a reduction in energy required to consume food. For example, the "slicing and pounding meat caused early humans to chew 17% less often and 26% less forcefully--saving as many as 2.5 million chews per year". With food processing requiring less energy to consume food, the energy has been used for other developments such as speech, thermoregulation, and even larger brain sizes. However, the smaller overall size of our jaws caused dental issues.
