# Detect water based damage due to worn out sealants

When encountering water based stains on a ceiling above a room, and the location coincides with the edges of the room, then the likely cause is due to worn out sealants. The room above will likely be a bathroom.

Cure the cause by resealing the room where appropriate and [[use-oil-based-stain-blockers-for-water-based-damage-and-vice-versa]].
