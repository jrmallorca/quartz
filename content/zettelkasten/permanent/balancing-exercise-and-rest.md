# Balancing exercise and rest

As [[resting-after-exercise-repairs-damage-to-body-and-builds-muscle]], achieving an optimum balance of the [[factors-involved-in-balancing-exercise-and-rest]] will have a [[result-of-optimum-balance-of-exercise-and-rest]].

If exercise is performed too much while resting too little, you will achieve the [[result-of-too-much-exercise-and-not-enough-rest]]. If exercise is too little while resting too much, you will achieve the [[result-of-too-much-rest-and-not-enough-exercise]].
