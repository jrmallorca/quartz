# Implementation intentions increase likelihood of responses through cues

The [[implementation-intention]] creates clarity towards a vague goal through becoming a concrete plan of action. It removes the need for motivation to strike.

For example, an [[implementation-intention]] changes the following from:

> I want to work out more.

to:

> I will work out at 12:00 in the living room.
