# Only advise when solicited

It's often tempting to immediately start giving out advice and potential solutions when a person is confiding in you their problems. However, [[when-someone-confides-in-you-assume-they-want-comfort-rather-than-solutions-until-proven-otherwise]]

To be less tempted to give away advice, reframe your knowledge and advice as something valuable enough to pay for.
