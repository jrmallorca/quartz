# Different contexts and environments can facilitate updates to current routine

As our [[behaviour-is-defined-by-our-relationships-to-objects-in-the-environment]], routines and [[habit]]s are easier to add or change in a different environment. There are no subtle [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] that may be present in your normal environment that would suggest you to continue your current habits.

For example, attempting to exercise at home would be different than attempting to exercise in the gym. At home, there would be many conflicting cues to the habit of exercise while the gym would have a context and many influences that induce the act of exercise.

A different context or environment could merely be the following:

- Cafe.
- Gym.
- Different, unused room in the house.
- Bench in the park.

If it's not easy to move into a different appropriate environment for your habit, then [[design-your-environment-for-your-habits]].
