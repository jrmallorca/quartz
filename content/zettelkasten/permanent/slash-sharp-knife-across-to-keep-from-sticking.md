# Slash sharp knife across to keep cut objects from sticking

To prevent objects being cut from sticking to the knife, use a slashing motion when cutting.

To do this:

1. Ensure the knife is sharp.
2. Put the tip of the knife away from you and across the object.
3. Drag the tip of the knife backwards until it has fully cut the object.
