# The word "Identity" means "repeated beingness" in Latin

"The word 'Identity' was originally derived from the Latin words 'essentitas', which means being, and 'identidem', which means repeatedly.

Your identity is literally your 'repeated beingness.'"

This resonates with [[identity-based-habits]] heavily. What you do can determine your identity.
