# Overpaying mortgage or saving can depend on interest rates

The choice of either overpaying the mortgage or saving the money to pay off the mortgage for later will depend on which choice has the higher interest rate.

If the mortgage interest rate is 5% and a savings account is 10%, then the money will be better used in the savings account as you will be able to get more money to pay off the mortgage and vice versa.
