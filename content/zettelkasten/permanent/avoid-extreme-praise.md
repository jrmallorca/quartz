# Avoid extreme praise

There are a few reasons we should avoid [[extreme-praise]]:

- [[extreme-praise-can-be-interpreted-as-having-low-standards-for-recipients]]
- [[extreme-praise-can-foster-a-fear-of-failure]]

Even if it was interpreted sincerely, it sets an extreme standard for the person to keep achieving to receive praise. Instead of a fostering a growth mindset, it can breed a fear of failure as anything that doesn't guarantee success will be avoided.
