# Squats

## Primary muscle targets

- Quadriceps (Front of thigh)
- Glutes (Butt)
    - Gluteus maximus
    - Gluteus medius
- Adductor magnus (inner thigh)
- Hamstrings
- Erector spinae
- External abdominal oblique

## Variations

## Form

![[squat-front-view-form-video.mp4]] ![[squat-side-view-form-video.mp4]]

- Keep the heels and inside edges of the feet in firm contact with the ground.
- Stick your chest out to extend your upper back.
- Contract your abs to keep your ribs down.
- Feet should be shoulder width (or wider) apart.
- Feet should be pointed forward or slightly turned out (but not more than 10-15 degrees).
- Stick your arms out directly in front of you, one hand on top of the other, and look just past your hands.

### Starting position

- Hips should be positioned vertically over heels, not stuck out forward/behind.
- Shoulders should be positioned vertically over hips.

### During exercise

- Move hips backward and move knees forward (simultaneously or hip-first)
- Keep spine in a neutral position while descending (no rounding).
- Push the knees out as you descend, as far as you can without letting the inside edge of your foot come off the ground.

### End position

- Spine straight from shoulders to coccyx (no rounding in the lower back, especially).
- Descend until the crease of your hip is below the top of your knee (or as deep as you can without rounding).
- Knees as far out as possible.
