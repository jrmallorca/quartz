# The perception of goal setting suffers from survivorship bias

Goal setting suffers from a serious case of [[survivorship-bias]] from "winners and losers" of a goal.

Winners and losers have the same goals and [[goals-are-for-direction-while-systems-are-for-progress]]. This means it is what the participants have done differently that allows them to achieve their desired results.
