# Oxytocin increases trust in humans

"[[oxytocin]] specifically affects an individual's willingness to accept social risks arising through interpersonal interactions. These results concur with animal research suggesting an essential role for [[oxytocin]] as a biological basis of prosocial approach behaviour."
