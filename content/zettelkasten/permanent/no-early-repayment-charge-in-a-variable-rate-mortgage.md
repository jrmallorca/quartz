# No early repayment charge in a variable-rate mortgage

In a variable-rate mortgage, there are no early repayment charges. That means that there is no limit when it comes to overpaying, unlike when the [[outstanding-mortgage-can-be-overpaid-by-10-percent-annually-within-the-fixed-interest-rate-date-range]].
