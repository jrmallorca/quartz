# Keep mouth as dry as possible when brushing teeth

When brushing your teeth, keep the mouth as dry as possible. This means the following tips should be followed:

- [[do-not-wet-toothbrush-head-when-brushing-teeth]]
- [[do-not-wet-toothpaste-when-brushing-teeth]]
- [[do-not-rinse-mouth-before-or-after-brushing-teeth]]

This will allow the fluoride to stick to the teeth and be at maximum effectiveness.
