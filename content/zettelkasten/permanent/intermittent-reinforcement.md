# Intermittent reinforcement

Intermittent reinforcement is a type of [[reinforcement-learning-in-animals]] that rewards behaviour occasionally instead of each time the behaviour is performed.

There are 4 types of intermittent reinforcement:

- [[fixed-interval-intermittent-reinforcement]]
- [[fixed-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]]
- [[variable-interval-intermittent-reinforcement]]
- [[variable-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]]
