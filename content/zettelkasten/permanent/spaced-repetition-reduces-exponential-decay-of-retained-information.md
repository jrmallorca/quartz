# Spaced repetition reduces exponential decay of retained information

[[spaced-repetition]] regularly refreshes the [[forgetting-curve]] of stored information while reducing its exponential decay; information is less likely to be forgotten with each session of active recall **when a bit of forgetting has occurred**.
