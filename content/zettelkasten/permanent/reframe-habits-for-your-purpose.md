# Reframe habits for your purpose

As a part of either [[make-the-craving-attractive]] or [[make-the-craving-unattractive]], reframe the habits you want to create/destroy for your purposes. Consider the following examples:

- Exercise
    - Typically this activity is undesirable as it is a lot of effort to exert and can be painful.
    - By re-framing it instead to view its benefits instead (e.g., as a way to gain skills, unlock activities you couldn't do before, etc.), it can improve our motivation to perform exercise.
- Alcohol
    - Typically this activity is desirable as it is a way to be social or to forget your troubles
    - By re-framing it instead to view its consequences instead (e.g., it destroys any fitness progressions, can ruin friendships, makes you feel lethargic and nauseous the next day, etc.), it can improve our motivation to quit drinking alcohol.
