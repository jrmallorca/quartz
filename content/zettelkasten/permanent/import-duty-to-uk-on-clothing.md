# Import duty to UK on clothing

For [[import-duty-to-uk]] regarding articles of clothing, there will likely be none when importing from EU. However, there will likely be import duty from USA.
## Flashcards

When importing **clothing** to UK from _North America_, will you likely have to pay import duty? :: Yes.

When importing **clothing** to UK from _countries in the EU_, will you likely have to pay import duty? :: No.
