# Bad habits are engineered to be superstimuli from corporations

The reason why it can be very hard to quit a bad habit is because corporations have mastered [[how-to-create-a-habit]]. These habits could be the following:

- food,
    - [[orosensation]]
- shopping,
    - [[diderot-effect]]
- porn,
- social media,
- video games,
- gambling,
    - [[variable-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]]
- etc.

Each one has become a [[superstimulus]] to achieve constant participation from its users. It introduces constant novelty and variation, and therefore [[dopamine]], to us that it is hard to get bored of these habits.
