# Interleaved practice through Zeigarnik effect

[[interleaved-practice]] can be performed through the [[zeigarnik-effect]] by creating deliberate breaks in a topic being studied.

It is more effective as the learner gets nearer to understanding or familiarising themselves with the topic as [[intensity-of-zeigarnik-effect-is-directly-correlated-to-progress-to-completion]].
