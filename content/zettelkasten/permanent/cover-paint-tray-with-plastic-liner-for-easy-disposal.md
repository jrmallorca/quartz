# Cover paint tray with plastic liner for easy disposal

Cover the paint tray with plastic bag or liner before you pour the paint. This will allow for easy disposal of the paint back to the container by closing the plastic bag and creating a hole to direct the paint into. Otherwise, it can be disposed of easier while not staining the paint tray.
