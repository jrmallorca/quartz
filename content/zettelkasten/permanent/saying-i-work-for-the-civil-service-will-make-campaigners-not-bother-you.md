# Saying "I work for the civil service" will make campaigners not bother you

To make campaigners go away and not bother you, whether you're outside or if someone knocks on your door, you can mention the following:

> "I work for the civil service".

The reason this works is because they understand that civil servants must appear impartial to political sides.

This technique should work even if you don't work for the civil service or if there's no upcoming election.
