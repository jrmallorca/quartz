# How to follow "Hara hachi bun me"

[[hara-hachi-bun-me]] can be achieved by eating until you start to feel full. This is to account for the fact that [[human-bodies-take-an-average-of-20-minutes-to-signal-the-stomach-satiety-from-the-first-bite]].
