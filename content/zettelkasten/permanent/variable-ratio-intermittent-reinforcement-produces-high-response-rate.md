# Variable ratio intermittent reinforcement produces high response rate

[[variable-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]] produces a high response rate due to its unpredictable nature.

An application of variable ratio intermittent reinforcement are slot machines or gacha machines in gambling. With such ease of repetition, variable rewards and getting the feeling of being "oh-so-close" to a reward makes it addictive.
