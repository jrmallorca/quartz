# Exercise beginners should focus on strength-based training

Beginners to exercise will gain more benefits from [[strength-based-training]], i.e., low reps and high sets. At this stage, muscle size and force output has a weak relationship.

Alternatively, [[experienced-athletes-should-focus-on-periodisation]].
