# Result of too much exercise and not enough rest

By exercising too much and resting too little, you will not be able to get the benefits of [[resting-after-exercise-repairs-damage-to-body-and-builds-muscle]] as there will be too much accumulated damage to the body. This results in:

- Decreased performance.
- Increased risk of injury.
