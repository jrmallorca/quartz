# Good habits are feedback loops for achieving desired results, reinforcing identity, and improving self-esteem

The formation of good habits is three-fold in its benefits:
- It achieves the desired results from the habit.
- [[identity-based-habits-are-a-feedback-loop-for-associated-identities]].
- It improves self-esteem.

These benefits do not occur only once; they happen for every successful act of the habit.
