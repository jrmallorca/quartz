# Consider minimising garments carried and washing if the maximum capacity of garments carried within 1 bag is less than the length of the holiday

If the maximum capacity of garments carried within 1 bag is less than the length of the holiday, then consider minimising the amount to bring and washing the garments instead.

For example, if you're unable to reasonably pack 30 t-shirts for a 30-day holiday, then reduce the amount of t-shirts and wash them during the holiday instead.
