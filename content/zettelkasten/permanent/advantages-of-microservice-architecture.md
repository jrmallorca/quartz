# Advantages of microservice architecture

As microservices are small and independently deployable:

- Finding bugs, confined in a service, is easier due to a reduction in complexity.
- Modification of an individual service won't require tests and redeployments of the whole service.
- Services can be individually scaled, reducing waste in cost and computing resources.

As microservices are loosely coupled, it facilitates any replacement of the service.
