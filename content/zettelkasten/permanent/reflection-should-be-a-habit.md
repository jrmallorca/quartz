# Reflection should be a habit

The act of [[reflection]] should be a [[habit]] itself, ideally [[habit-stacking]] on top of a [[habit]] we want to improve on. This is because [[reflection-improves-habits]]

## Concrete example

During each exercise, we film ourselves to check [[correct-form-in-exercise]]. After the exercise, we review the video to see how we can correct ourselves further.
