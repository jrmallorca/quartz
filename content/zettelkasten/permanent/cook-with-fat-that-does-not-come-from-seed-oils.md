# Cook with fat that does not come from seed oils

For cooking that requires high smoke points, e.g., stir-frying, shallow-frying, deep-frying, etc., use either:

- Avocado oil
- Ghee

For cooking that doesn't require high smoke points, you could use:

- Avocado oil
- Ghee
- Animal fat (e.g., tallow, dripping, lard, goose fat, duck fat)
- Extra virgin olive oil (only for low heat)

Note that while ghee is a type of animal fat, it deserves a special mention due to its high smoke point.
