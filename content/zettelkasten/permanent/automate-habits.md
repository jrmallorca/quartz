# Automate habits

Automation is the ultimate end of a habit. The benefits from [[good-habits-are-feedback-loops-for-achieving-desired-results-reinforcing-identity-and-improving-self-esteem]] are maximised when automated.

Consider the following examples:
- The 2nd example of the [[commitment-device]], which involves buying smaller lunch boxes, will automatically reduce the amount of food to eat and can help with dieting.
- Robot vacuums which automate a part of cleaning spaces. This makes cleaning a space easier as it reduces the effort, time, and cognition to clean.
- Standing orders and multiple accounts/pots automate budgeting. Standing orders ensure the budget has been allocated to the correct account/pot while minimising the chance of tampering with the budget.

Each example will [[make-the-response-easy]] as [[habit-formation-minimises-cognitive-load-usage-for-other-activities]] and [[behaviour-is-defined-by-our-relationships-to-objects-in-the-environment]].
