# Help to Buy - Wales equity loan interest rate increase calculation

The formula for interest rate increase calculation is the following:

$$
\begin{align}
IR  &= \text{Interest rate} \\
CPI &= \text{Consumer Price Index} \\
NIR &= \text{New interest rate} \\
\\
NIR &= IR \times (CPI + 2\%)
\end{align}
$$
