# Goodhart's Law

Goodhart's Law states the following:

> When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.

In the [Rethinking Service Blog](https://rethinkingservice.blogspot.com/2018/05/9-reasons-why-targets-make-performance.html), we can see many instances of how focusing solely on meeting targets that came from measures can produce unintended side effects just to meet them.
