# Identity based habits are a double edged sword

Good [[identity-based-habits]] make it easy to follow as [[identity-can-grow-intrinsic-motivation-for-habits]]. Unfortunately, this also makes it easy to unconsciously follow habits associated with a bad identity.

Examples:
- I'm bad with directions
- I'm not a morning person
