# Cravings stems from the change in state not the habit

With [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]], the desire is not performing the [[habit]] itself but the change in state it delivers.

Consider the following examples:

- You don't want to turn on the television, you want to be entertained.
- You don't want to brush your teeth, you want to feel a clean mouth.
- You don't crave for snacks, you want relief/distraction from the stress you're feeling.
