# Victimisation

Victimisation is defined by the [[equality-act-2010]] as treating someone badly or different as they have either:

- Done a [[protected-act-in-terms-of-equality-act-2010]]
- Believe a person has done or is going to do a [[protected-act-in-terms-of-equality-act-2010]].

## Analogy

## Concrete example

- Labelled a troublemaker.
- Withheld promotions or job opportunities.
- Being left out.
- Bullying behaviour after a complaint was made.
