# Disadvantages of monolithic architecture

- Modification of anything in a [[monolithic-architecture]] will likely require retesting and redeployment due to its tightly-coupled nature.
- Scaling a feature would require scaling the whole codebase, wasting costs and compute resources.
- Replacement of any features is harder due to features being tightly-coupled.

Overall, it's harder to maintain as it grows larger.
