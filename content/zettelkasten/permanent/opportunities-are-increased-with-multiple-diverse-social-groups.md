# Opportunities are increased with multiple diverse social groups

There are increased amounts of opportunities the more **diverse** friends and acquaintances are between social groups/circles, i.e., people who aren't in the same social groups/circles.

Friends and acquaintances that come from different social groups have information or opportunities that are less likely to overlap with each other.

## Concrete example

For example, if your set of friends are mainly health workers, then you're mainly going to get opportunities related to health. Whereas if you have friends from different social circles (e.g., rich, computer science, health, finance), then the likelihood that the amount of opportunities in general will increase.
