# Association with identity does not require perfection for success

To associate with an identity, the harmonious habits only need to win the majority of the time against the conflicting habits.

[[streaks-can-trigger-the-abstinence-violation-effect-if-broken]]. While [[identity-based-habits-are-a-feedback-loop-for-associated-identities]], breaking the [[streak]] doesn't remove all the progress made to maintain the identity.

Don't let perfect be the enemy of good.
