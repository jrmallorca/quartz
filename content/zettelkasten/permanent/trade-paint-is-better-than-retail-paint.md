# Trade paint is better than retail paint

Trade paint is much better than the retail version. While a bit more expensive, it's highly likely to cost less financially and physically in the long run due to the following reasons:

- Trade paint has better opacity and is more likely to only need 2 coats.
- Trade paint has better coverage for each roll in the wall you do.
- Trade paint is more durable due to better materials being used.
- Trade paint's finish is either equal or better than retail paint, but never worse.
