# Food with high dynamic contrast are superstimuli

A high [[dynamic-contrast-in-food]] can be considered a [[superstimulus]]. It keeps the experience novel and interest which encourages us to eat more as [[sensory-specific-satiety]] is slower.

For example, imagine a warm chocolate brownie paired with vanilla ice cream. The amount of different sensations you could feel from the dish could be:

- The temperature contrast between the warm brownie and cold ice cream.
- The textural contrast between the brownie and ice cream.
- The textural contrast of the crunchy exterior of the brownie and the gooey interior.
- The complementary tastes of the chocolate brownie and vanilla ice cream.

This is in contrast to [[food-with-low-dynamic-contrast-can-contribute-to-quicker-satiety]].
