# Golden ratio

The golden ratio is an irrational number that is considered to be present in physical designs considered aesthetically pleasing.

## Calculation

The golden ratio is calculated as:

$$
\frac{a + b}{a} = \frac{a}{b} = 1.618... = \phi
$$

Consider this analogy:

- Divide a line into two parts so that:
  - the whole length divided by the long part is also equal to
  - the long part divided by the short part.
