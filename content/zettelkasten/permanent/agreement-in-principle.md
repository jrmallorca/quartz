# Agreement in principle (AIP)

According to [this article](https://www.which.co.uk/money/mortgages-and-property/mortgages/getting-a-mortgage/mortgage-agreements-in-principle-aips-aekVS6J9x06b), an agreement in principle (AIP) is a certificate or statement from a lender which states that, 'in principle', they would lend you a certain amount.
