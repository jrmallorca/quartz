# How to toggle water input to tank for flushing at toilets

To toggle the water tank to be refilled for flushing a toilet:

1. Go underneah the toilet bowl.
2. Find the flat cap screw.
	1. When the flat cap screw is horizontal, water will refill the tank for flushing.
	2. When the flat cap screw is vertical, water will not refill the tank for flushing.
3. Turn the screw 90° to toggle the water input to the tank for flushing.
