# Premack's principle can cause classical (Pavlovian) conditioning

[[premacks-principle]] can cause [[classical-pavlovian-conditioning]].

Consider the 1st example in [[premacks-principle-is-also-known-as-grandmas-rule]]:

- To have 1 hour of gaming ([[mpb-in-premacks-principle]]), you must complete your homework ([[lpb-in-premacks-principle]]).
    - The [[unconditioned-stimulus] is gaming.
    - The [[unconditioned-response] is either fun or a reduction in stress.
    - With repetition, the homework is a [[neutral-stimulus]] that becomes the [[conditioned-stimulus]].

However, the effect of conditioning depends on the following:

- [[temporal-gap-between-mpb-lpb-pairing-in-premacks-principle-determines-classical-conditioning-efficacy]]
- [[competing-associations-with-mpb-lpb-pairing-in-premacks-principle-determines-classical-conditioning-efficacy]]
- [[motivation-for-mpb-determines-efficacy-of-premacks-principle]]
- [[motivation-for-lpb-determines-requirement-of-premacks-principle]]
