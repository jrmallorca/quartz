# Goals are at odds with long-term progress

After a goal has been accomplished, often the motivation to continue the systems requires to achieve it disappears. This reverts people back to their old habits. [[maintenance-of-results-requires-maintenance-of-systems]]. This reinforces that [[goals-are-for-direction-while-systems-are-for-progress]].

"The purpose of setting goals is to win the game. The purpose of building systems is to continue playing the game. True long-term thinking is goal-less thinking."
