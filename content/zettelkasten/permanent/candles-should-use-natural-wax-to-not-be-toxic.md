# Candles should use natural wax to not be toxic

When buying candles, ensure that the material is made of a natural wax to not be toxic. For example:

- Soy wax
- Bees wax
- Coconut wax

Avoid the following:

- Paraffin wax
- Any wax that mentions "blend".
