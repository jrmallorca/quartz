# Difference between lapse and relapse

In terms of prohibited activities, the difference between [[lapse-in-terms-of-prohibited-activities]] and [[relapse-in-terms-of-prohibited-activities]] is that a lapse is a momentary slip while a relapse is a prolonged return to the prohibited activity.
