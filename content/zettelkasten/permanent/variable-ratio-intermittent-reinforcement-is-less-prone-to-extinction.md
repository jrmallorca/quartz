# Variable ratio intermittent reinforcement is less prone to extinction

[[variable-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]] will take significantly more activity before quitting it, versus [[fixed-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement]], due to its unpredictable nature and [[variable-ratio-intermittent-reinforcement-produces-high-response-rate]].

If a gambling machine, an application of variable ratio intermittent reinforcement, was broken, the lack of results are likely to be interpreted as "a streak of bad luck" rather than recognising it's broken.
