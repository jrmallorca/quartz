# Marination is mainly a surface treatment

Except for salt, most components within a marinade is only a surface-level treatment. This is because of a number of factors:

- Most components of the marindes have molecules that are too big to penetrate the meat, unlike salt which only has 2 atoms: sodium and chlorine.
- [[meat-is-approximately-75-percent-water]]. There's not much more room for any more liquid to soak in.
- Most marinades use oil. [[oil-and-water-do-not-mix]].
