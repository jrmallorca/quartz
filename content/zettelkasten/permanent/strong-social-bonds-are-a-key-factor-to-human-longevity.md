# Strong social bonds are a key factor to human longevity

Strong social bonds and [[interpersonal-interaction]] are part of the [[key-factors-of-human-longevity]] due to the following factors:

- Stress from isolation is minimised.
- Induces healthy behaviour
- Induces cognitive stimulation as referred to [[cognitive-stimulation-is-fundamental-for-a-healthy-lifestyle]].

It is similar to how [[ikigai-is-a-key-factor-to-human-longevity]].
