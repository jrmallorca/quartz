# Recording habit streaks should be habit stacked

Recording a [[streak]] should undergo [[habit-stacking]] after the completion of the [[zettelkasten/permanent/habit]]. This effectively makes it a [[zettelkasten/permanent/habit]] itself.

The reason why is because [[streaks-are-effective-motivators]] and can [[make-an-immediately-satisfying-reward-for-a-habit-without-one]]. It also helps to not forget the details of the performance when tracking.
