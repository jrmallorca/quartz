# Value dimension in colour season

The value dimension of the [[colour-season]] determines the intensity of the colours present in the skin, hair, and eyes of the face, especially in the hair and eyes.

In terms of people, this would determine how light or dark a person's hair and eyes are versus the average of their ethnicity.

An example of the light end of the spectrum, imagine a white person with light blonde hair and pale blue eyes.

An example of the dark end of the spectrum, imagine an far-eastern Asian person with white skin, black hair, and black eyes.
