# Habits can have multiple cues

A [[habit]] can have multiple [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]]. This makes the habit a lot more likely to be performed. This could make it subject to [[identity-based-habits-are-a-feedback-loop-for-associated-identities]].

Consider a smoker and their potential cues to smoke. The cues could be any of the following:

- Driving in the car.
- Seeing a friend smoke.
- Feeling stressed.
