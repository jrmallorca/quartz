# Simplicity is a proxy for understanding

It is easy to commit terms into memory for specific topics, but memorisation is not equal to understanding. When specific terminology cannot be used, explanations of a topic must follow [[atomicity]] and be broken down to ideally its most basic form to achieve true understanding.

Therefore, simplicity is a proxy for understanding.

The following are related to breaking down topics to more basic forms:
