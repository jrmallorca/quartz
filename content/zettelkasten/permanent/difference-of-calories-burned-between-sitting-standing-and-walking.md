# Difference of calories burned between sitting, standing, and walking

In a study with 74 healthy people, the folloing conclusions were found:

- While sitting, study subjects burned 80 calories/hour.
- [[standing-only-burns-slightly-more-calories-than-sitting]]; the number of calories burned was only about 88 calories/hour
- Walking burned 210 calories/hour.
