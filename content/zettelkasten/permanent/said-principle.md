# SAID principle

1. Your muscular system does not like working harder than it needs to.
2. Your body overall does not like using more resources than it needs to.

This means allocating resources to only the parts of the body required to perform an action. The allocation of resources can entail their development if required, causing our muscles to get bigger, stronger, flexible, more stamina, etc.

It will not allocate or maintain any parts not required to perform an action.

These are the foundations of **S**pecific **A**daptations to **I**mposed **D**emands, the **SAID** principle.
