# Beginners to exercise will benefit more from newbie gains than periodisation

Those who still experience [[newbie-gains]] would find it more beneficial to get used to exercising rather than focusing on a specific goal through [[periodisation]]. This is because the muscles developed through  [[newbie-gains]] come through from mere usage and will aid in getting more of the body used to activity again.

[[periodisation]] will be more useful once the body has become accustomed to activity and requires more effort to develop certain muscles for specific activities. We travel closer to the [[law-of-diminishing-returns]]
