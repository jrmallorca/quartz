# Staircasing Help to Buy - Wales equity loan

For [[staircasing]] of the [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]], the loan could be paid off in full or partly anytime before the loan term.

Any part repayments must be at least 10% of property value at the time of repayment, NOT the value of the home at the time of purchase.

Staircasing cannot be performed if there are any outstanding interest payments, management fees, etc.
