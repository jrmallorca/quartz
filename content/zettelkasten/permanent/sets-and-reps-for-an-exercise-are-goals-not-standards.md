# Sets and reps for an exercise are goals, not standards

When establishing a certain amount of sets and reps for an exercise, it is a goal to achieve. Once the goal has been achieved, higher weights must be used for [[progressive-overload]].

Consider the following: if we're establishing 3 sets and 8 reps for pull-ups, then we can achieve 8 reps on the 1st set, 5 reps on the 2nd set, and 3 reps on the 3rd set. Until we achieve a consistent 8 reps for all 3 sets, then we continuously pursue that goal. Once that goal has been achieved, then must increase the weight. Following the [[said-principle]], do not keep it at the same weight unless you merely want to maintain that level of performance.

This applies for both strength-based training and hypertrophy-based training.
