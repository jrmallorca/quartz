# Unhealthy sun exposure during the day in the UK is between 11:00 - 15:00

In the UK, the sun is strongest between 11:00 - 15:00 from March to October. Therefore, sun exposure before and after this time period will be healthy and produce vitamin D without ultraviolet rays being too harsh as [[ultraviolet-rays-uva-uvb-are-present-every-day]].
