# Situations in stories should describe people

Stories are better when they're about people rather than products. Therefore, tell a little more about the main character and what is on their mind.

- What do they want in this specific situation?
- What are they afraid of?
- What are they excited about?

## Analogy

## Concrete example

- “I was a little surprised to have him on the phone, but then I thought ‘This is great. I can now share the newest innovation from our R&D department. He’ll be super impressed.’”
- “I was tired of life. It had been a very hectic year and I was ready for a break.”
- “He felt good about the week. If he kept that performance up, he’d be promoted to partner in no time.”
