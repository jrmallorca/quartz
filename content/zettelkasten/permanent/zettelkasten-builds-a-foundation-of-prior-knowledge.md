# Zettelkasten builds a foundation of prior knowledge

[[zettelkasten]] facilitates [[learning-requires-a-foundation-of-prior-knowledge]] through [[atomicity-in-zettelkasten]].

The process of [[zettelkasten]] involves creating atomic [[zettelkasten-literature-notes-represent-formed-information-about-extracted-concepts-from-fleeting-notes]] to be connected through [[zettelkasten-permanent-notes-represent-literature-notes-linked-with-permanent-notes]]. This process of connecting atomic notes to each other is the creation of a foundation of prior knowledge in an external store of information.

## Concrete example

Every single note in our zettelkasten is a small part in our foundation of knowledge.
