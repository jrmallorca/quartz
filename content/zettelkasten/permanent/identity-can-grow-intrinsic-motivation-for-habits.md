# Identity can grow intrinsic motivation for habits

Forming an identity will grow intrinsic motivation to create or maintain habits that associates with the identity, i.e., [[identity-based-habits]].

(As an example, think about the time I broke the Linux install on the SSD. One of my core identities is being a Linux user and to not have a Linux install would be devastatingly impactful to the identity. I quickly resolved that and made further protections to prevent the incident happening again.)

"Doing the right thing is easy. After all, when your behaviour and your identity are fully aligned, you are no longer pursuing behaviour change. You are simply acting like the type of person you already believe yourself to be."
