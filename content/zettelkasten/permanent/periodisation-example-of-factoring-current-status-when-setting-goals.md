# Periodisation example of factoring current status when setting goals

An example of [[factor-your-current-status-when-setting-goals]] in terms of [[periodisation]] is having a goal for a front lever.

A person who is comfortable on the bar and plenty of training for muscles used during a front lever will have a different set of goals versus someone who could barely do a pull up.
