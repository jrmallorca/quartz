# Habit - Change ceiling shade

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Acquired new ceiling shade.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Turn off the light where you will place the shade.
2. Remove light bulb.
3. Hold the top of the light fitting and remove the collar at the bottom by turning it counter-clockwise.
4. Remove the current shade, if any.
5. Insert the new shade in.
6. Insert the collar back by turning it clockwise until tight.
7. Insert light bulb.
