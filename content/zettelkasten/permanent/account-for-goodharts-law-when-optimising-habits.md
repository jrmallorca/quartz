# Account for Goodhart's Law when optimising habits

When we start to [[organise-standardise-optimise]] our habits and create metrics (e.g., [[streak]]), ensure to account for [[goodharts-law]].

When a measure starts to be used as a target for optimisation, we may get unintended and/or undesirable side effects. Optimising the wrong measurement, if there's even a right measurement, may result in achieving the wrong behaviour.
