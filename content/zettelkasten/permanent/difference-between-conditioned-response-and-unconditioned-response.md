# Difference between conditioned response and unconditioned response

While the [[similarity-between-conditioned-response-and-unconditioned-response]] is the behaviour, the difference is that a [[conditioned-response]] comes from the [[conditioned-stimulus]] and the [[unconditioned-response]] comes from the [[unconditioned-stimulus]].
