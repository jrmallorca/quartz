# Focusing on systems rather than goals does not restrict happiness

[[setting-goals-can-restrict-your-happiness-until-its-achievement]]. However, when you fall in love with the process rather than the product, i.e. [[focus-on-systems-not-goals]], you don’t have to wait to give yourself permission to be happy. You can be satisfied anytime your system is running.
