# Outcome-based habits are not ineffective

While [[identity-based-habits]] are more advocated for, [[outcome-based-habits]] are still useful to achieve a specific goal. Remember that [[goals-are-for-direction-while-systems-are-for-progress]] and we can think of [[periodisation]] as its example.

Are _outcome-based habits_ **ineffective** VERSUS _identity-based habits_? :: No.^1713988592486
