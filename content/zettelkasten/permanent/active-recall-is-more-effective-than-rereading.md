# Active recall is more effective than rereading

Active recall such as [[spaced-repetition]] is more effective as a learning strategy than review by rereading, an application of [[massed-practice]]. [[spaced-repetition-reduces-exponential-decay-of-retained-information]] while [[massed-practice-gives-the-illusion-of-mastery]].
