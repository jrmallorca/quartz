# Provisional Replacement Certificate (PRC) can be used to substitute missing UK GHIC

If you need state-funded treatment while visiting another country and do not have your [[uk-global-health-insurance-card-ghic]], a Provisional Replacement Certificate (PRC) can be requested after treatment to have the same cover as the card.
