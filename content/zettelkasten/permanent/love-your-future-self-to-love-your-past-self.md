# Love your future self to love your past self

In essence, this means performing actions for the betterment of your future self.

This could be seen in the following:
- [[restarting-exercise-via-body-weight-fitness]] for your body appearance and capability.
- [[zettelkasten-and-spaced-repetition]] for learning.
- Backing up your data to minimise the damage of a device being gone.
- [[personal-care-products]] for body appearance and health.
- [[key-factors-of-human-longevity]]
- [[identity-based-habits]]

Especially when these results are tangible and observed, it will make your future self love your past self. [[good-habits-are-feedback-loops-for-achieving-desired-results-reinforcing-identity-and-improving-self-esteem]]
