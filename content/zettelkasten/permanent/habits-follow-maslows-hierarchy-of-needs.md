# Habits follow "Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs"

Each [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] ultimately stems from predicting a reward from [[maslows-hierarchy-of-needs]].

Meanwhile, a [[craving-in-terms-of-habits]] is a specific manifestation of the primal need (e.g., sustenance, stress relief, reproduction, etc.).

As an example, imagine noticing some cue that activates a craving for a taco. The deeper, underlying motive may be needing to eat food to survive.
