# The enemy of a good plan is the dream of a perfect plan

"The enemy of a good plan is the dream of a perfect plan."
    - Prussian General Karl von Clausewitz, Vom Kriege, 1832

I like this quote much better than "Perfect is the enemy of good" as there is more context behind why perfect can be the enemy of good.

This can relate to [[fear-of-failure-can-stagnate-progress]] as attempts to concoct a perfect plan, the organise or optimise step of [[organise-standardise-optimise]], can paralyse you from action. The action is where progress actually happens.
