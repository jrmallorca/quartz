# Sperm production rate is affected by heat

The reason testicles are outside of the male body is because they prefer to be slightly cooler than the rest of the body. This is essential for sperm production and testicular health. Therefore, anything that would subject the testicles with more heat is bad.

Examples:
- [[wearing-tight-underwear-slightly-reduces-sperm-production]]
- Using a laptop on your lap.
- Saunas.
- Prolonged sitting.
- Obesity
