# Privilege is merely a synonym for systemic advantage

Privilege is not meant as an insult or a way to remove the effort from what you earned; it merely means an advantage a person or group experiences, usually due to institutions or systems put in place that allow for that (e.g., class, wealth, skin, etc.). A privilege just means that an obstacle either did not exist, or was easier to face, due to its existence.

We can liken this to cheats in games. If we use Sifu as an example, a privilege might be to turn on "Golden Weapon" cheat which will make your weapon unbreakable. That will then facilitate defeating enemies versus going bare-handed.

For an advantage to qualify as a privilege, it must stem from the overall systems or institutions in place. Consider the following examples:

- If a man and woman of the same capability were up for hire and the man got picked **because** of their gender, then it is a privilege.
- If a man and woman of the same capability were up for hire, but the gender and name was not known to the hirers, and the man got picked, then it is equality.
- If a man and woman of the same capability were up for hire and the woman got picked **because** of their gender (e.g., diversity hire), then it is a privilege.
- If an Asian or someone black walked into a store during COVID-19 times and the Asian was abused, then it is a privilege to be black.
    - [Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2021/08/talk-about-privilege-at-work)
- If a poor person had to save to buy a Versace garment and a celebrity were to just be gifted it, then it is a privilege to be famous/rich.
