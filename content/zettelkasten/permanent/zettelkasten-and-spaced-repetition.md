# Zettelkasten and Spaced Repetition

[[zettelkasten]] and [[spaced-repetition]] can be used harmoniously due to both following their own version of the [[atomicity]].

[[zettelkasten]] is focused on learning. [[spaced-repetition]] is focused on memorisation. However, it is important to [[learn-before-you-memorise]] which brings me to the idea of combining their usage.
