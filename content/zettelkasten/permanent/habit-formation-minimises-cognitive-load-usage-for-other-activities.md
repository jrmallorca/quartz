# Habit formation minimises cognitive load usage for other activities

As [[habit-formation-naturally-optimises-behaviour]], the cognitive load will be minimised and can be directed towards other beneficial activities. Refer to the [[importance-of-leaving-the-comfort-zone-for-cognition]].

Alternatively, this automaticity can make us overlook the consequences of a bad habit.

Ultimately, this aligns with [[neuroplasticity]].
