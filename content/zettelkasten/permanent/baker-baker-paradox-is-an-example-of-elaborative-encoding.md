# Baker-baker paradox is an example of elaborative encoding

[[baker-baker-paradox]] is an application of [[elaborative-encoding]]. The likely reason for a person being able to remember that someone is a baker, versus someone is named Baker, is that the profession has more associations to it than just the name.

For example, the baker profession can conjure images of the smell of bread, funny hat, etc. while the name Baker is stand-alone.

The reason why [[baker-baker-paradox]] is an application of [[elaborative-encoding]] is that the metadata from the profession attaches itself and helps with retaining information about a person.
