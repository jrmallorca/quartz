# Help to Buy - Wales equity loan helps with monthly repayments

In terms of monthly repayments, [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]] will be quite a boon as it will likely lower mortgage repayments, due to [[lower-loan-to-value-ratio-decreases-mortgage-interest-rate]], as well as be interest free for 5 years and start off with a low interest rate of 1.75% at year 6. This is based on [[details-of-help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]].

Assuming CPI doesn't increase too much from 2029, the [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan-interest-rate-increase-calculation]] shouldn't make it compound at a crazy level.
