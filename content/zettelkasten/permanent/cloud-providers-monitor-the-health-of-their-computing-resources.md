# Cloud providers monitor the health of their computing resources

As [[cloud-providers-only-guarantee-availability-for-systems-they-cover]], they will have systems in place to monitor the health of their computing resources, such as [[virtual-machine]]s and any in [[cloud-providers-already-have-infrastructure]], and recover any unhealthy ones. This is to prevent an [[example-of-a-cloud-system-outage]].
