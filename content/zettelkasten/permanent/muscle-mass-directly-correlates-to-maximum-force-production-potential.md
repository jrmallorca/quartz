# Muscle mass directly correlates to maximum force production potential

Muscle mass directly correlates to **maximum** force production potential.

It is important to note that maximum force production potential is the maximum potential of how powerful a muscle is at a given size. It is not the current maximum force output in a single rep.

This explicitly states "maximum" as larger muscles can be as powerful/weak as a smaller muscle with better quality.
