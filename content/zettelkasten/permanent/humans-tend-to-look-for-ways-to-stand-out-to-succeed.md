# Humans tend to look for ways to stand out to succeed

After someone [[join-a-group-that-fosters-your-desired-identity]], they will tend to look for various ways to stand out from the group to be admired and respected. This is observed in [[maslows-hierarchy-of-needs-4th-layer]].

This can involve copying the behaviour of successful people.
