# Use medium pile roller sleeves in interior walls

Medium pile roller sleeves can be used in interior walls; it's what all the tradesmen use due to the folloeing reasons below:

- Soft pile barely gets anything on before you have to refill.
- [[using-long-pile-roller-sleeves-to-paint-smooth-walls-results-in-orange-peel-texture]]

Medium pile gives the best compromise between coverage and smoothness of application.
