# Consistency of MPB-LPB pairing in Premack's principle determines classical conditioning efficacy

For [[premacks-principle-can-cause-classical-conditioning]] to happen, one of the factors is the consistency of the pairing between the [[mpb-in-premacks-principle]] and [[lpb-in-premacks-principle]].

Consider the 1st example in [[premacks-principle-is-also-known-as-grandmas-rule]]:

- To have 1 hour of gaming ([[mpb-in-premacks-principle]]), you must complete your homework ([[lpb-in-premacks-principle]]).

For [[classical-pavlovian-conditioning]] to take effect, the pairing must be consistent. If the reward of gaming is not guaranteed after completing homework, the formation of a [[conditioned-stimulus]] may not occur.
