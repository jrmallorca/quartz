# Considered method to balance Help to Buy - Wales equity loan advantages and disadvantages

Perhaps there is a balance to be achieved by just taking the minimum 10% while using a 5% deposit. [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan-helps-with-monthly-repayments]]; we can lower mortgage payments and have a relatively low interest rate from the loan after 5 years. There is still the issue of [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan-lump-sum-repayments-will-hurt]] by paying off AT LEAST £30,000 within say ~7 years. But it sounds achievable to save up for that lump sum within that time-frame, assuming our sources of income are stable.
