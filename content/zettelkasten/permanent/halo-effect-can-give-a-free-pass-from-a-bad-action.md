# Halo effect can give a free pass from a bad action

The [[halo-effect]] can mitigate any bad actions committed by a person with it. Either there would be disbelief that the action is committed by the person with the halo or the punishment would be reduced.

**Example**
- A person, named Andrew, can be subject to a halo where they're a good-natured person because they're handsome.
- Andrew proceeds to steal something.
- Another person who knows of Andrew may not easily believe that Andrew stole something as they believe them to be good-natured, despite not proving that behaviour initially.
- Alternatively, it would reduce the punishment that would usually be associated with the behaviour.
