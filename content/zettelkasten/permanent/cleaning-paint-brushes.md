# Cleaning paint brushes

When cleaning a paint brush, always have the brush side facing down. This prevents paint from getting inside the metal part, drying, and ruining the brush.

Use warm water.

Massage the brush until it looks clean. Once it looks clean, flatten the side of the brush onto your hand to force the remaining paint out. Do this until the water running through the brush is clear.

Clean the brush after 20 - 30 minutes of painting. This prevents the paint from drying and ruining the brush.
