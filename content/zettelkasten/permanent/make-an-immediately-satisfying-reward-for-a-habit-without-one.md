# Make an immediately satisfying reward for a habit without one

When attempting to create a [[habit]] where its rewards are not immediately satisfying or tangible, create a situation where you can.

Often these come in the form of [[premacks-principle]]. Consider the following examples:

- You have a goal of saving enough money to afford a holiday.
    - When we walk instead of taking the bus, we can put the amount of a bus ticket into holiday savings as a tangible reward.
    - When we skip getting a Starbucks coffee, we can put the amount of the coffee into holiday savings as a tangible reward.
    - Even when we exercise, we could reward ourself an arbitrarily chosen amount of money (e.g., £5 per exercise session).
- After an exercise, treat yourself to a massage/spa.

However, [[ensure-artificial-immediately-satisfying-rewards-to-habits-do-not-conflict-with-its-identity]] and follow[[rewards-from-gamification]].
