# Eligibility for Help to Buy - Wales equity loan

- New-build house.
- An eligible house from a [builder who is registered with the scheme](https://www.gov.wales/help-buy-wales-participating-builders).
- Maximum price of £300,000.
- Fund at least 80% of the property price through a combination of a repayment mortgage and a minimum deposit.
- Take out a first charge repayment mortgage with a [qualifying lender](https://www.gov.wales/help-buy-wales-participating-lenders).
- Minimum deposit of 5% of purchase price.
