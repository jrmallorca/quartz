# Habit stacking

Habit stacking is a variant of the [[implementation-intention]] where [[time-and-location-are-the-2-most-common-cues]] are implicitly embedded into the intention.

Habit stacking involves adding a new [[habit]] to your current routine using the completion of a current [[habit]] as the [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]].

> After <CURRENT_HABIT>, I will <NEW_HABIT>
