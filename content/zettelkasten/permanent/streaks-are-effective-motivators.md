# Streaks are effective motivators

[[streak]]s are effective motivators for [[habit]]s as it can serve to induce motivation or a sense of responsibility to continue the [[streak]], i.e., [[make-the-craving-attractive]]. It can also [[make-the-reward-satisfying]] as it can [[make-an-immediately-satisfying-reward-for-a-habit-without-one]].

Effectively, a [[streak]] provides the benefits in [[good-habits-are-feedback-loops-for-achieving-desired-results-reinforcing-identity-and-improving-self-esteem]]. It creates a visual and tangible representation of an identity.
