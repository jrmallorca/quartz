# Organise, standardise, optimise

"Organise, standardise, optimise" refers to a 3 steps to creation and perfection of a process.

The organise step refers to knowing enough to perform a process correctly such that a result can be achieved.

The standardise step refers to consistently achieving the result from a process.

The optimise step refers to improving the process to maximise reward and/or get closer toward the desired result.

## Flashcards

What does **"organise, standardise, optimise"** refer to? :: Creation and perfection of a process.^1719472881735

In _"organise, standardise, optimise"_, what is the **"organise"** step? :: The gathering of knowledge to perform a process for a result.^1719472881749

In _"organise, standardise, optimise"_, what is the **"standardise"** step? :: Assurance of consistency of getting a result with a given process.^1719472881761

In _"organise, standardise, optimise"_, what is the **"optimise"** step? :: Improvement of process to maximise reward.^1719472881784

Why should we FOLLOW _"organise, standardise, optimise"_ **in order**? :: Standardisation can never be achieved without knowledge of the process and result; Optimisation can never be achieved without lessons from standardisation.^1719472881801
