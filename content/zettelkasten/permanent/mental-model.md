# Mental model

A mental model is a compilation of our knowledge into a highly efficient cognitive framework or physical skill in an attempt to simplify encounters to new, old, or complex information from reality.

[[choosing-mental-models-requires-extraction-of-underlying-principles]]. Once the detection of a [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] for the [[mental-model]] is automatic, then it has become a [[habit]].

## Analogy

It is similar to [[analogies]] where it is only applicable as a comparison to reality up to a certain limit, until it breaks down as the complexity of reality becomes more complex.

## Concrete examples

### Physical skills

- Seeing and performing a curveball throw in baseball
- Typing anything on a normal keyboard
- Typing anything on a split keyboard
- [[habit-temper-wok]]
- [[habit-season-wok]]

### Knowledge structure mental models

- A memorised sequence of chess moves.
- Programming design patterns
