# Identities must not monopolise beliefs

Identities must not have a monopoly in your beliefs; [[the-best-version-of-yourself-requires-continuous-reflection]].

Letting certain identities monopolise beliefs will restrict adaptability when life changes us (e.g., if one is vegan but a health condition requires a change in diet). It will be like losing one's [[ikigai]], as said in [[ensure-your-ikigai-is-an-identity-rather-than-a-goal]].
