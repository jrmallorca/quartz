# Feynman technique simplifies explanations for better understanding

The [[feynman-technique]] involves the refinement of understanding of a topic through simplifying initial explanations as [[simplicity-is-a-proxy-for-understanding]]. This can be achieved through creating simpler explanations through analogies as they are easier to recall and explain.
