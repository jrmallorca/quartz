# Delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS)

Delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) is the pain and stiffness experienced in the muscles 24 - 72 hours after unaccustomed exercise. This typically occurs when a beginner works out and reduces in frequency and intensity with consistent exercise.
