# Genetics do not determine destiny

Genetics do not determine our destiny; they determine areas of opportunity. We can use the following:

- [[explore-exploit-trade-off-for-personal-success]].
- [[specialisation]].
- [[combine-talents-to-become-better-in-a-field]]
- Creation of a new field
    - This could be similar to the above suggestion, but could also be different such as making quantum physics separate from traditional physics.

The 1st suggestion has us explore our talents, while the last 3 suggestions narrow the field of competition for us to be better.

Genetics cannot make us successful if we're not doing the work. Limitations, fictitious or not, make us rarely exert the effort required to get close to them. [[quality-of-performance-can-plateau-or-decrease-without-nurture]].
