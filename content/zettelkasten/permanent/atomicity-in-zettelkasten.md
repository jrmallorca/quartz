# Atomicity in Zettelkasten

The principle of [[atomicity]] is important when taking notes, as seen in the [[minimum-information-principle]]. In [[zettelkasten]] the unit is a thought. A thought can consist of an idea, an argument or a concept, etc.

## Flashcards

What is the type of **unit** used in _Zettelkasten_? :: A thought.^1674412249407

What can a **thought consist of** in _Zettelkasten_? :: Ideas, arguments or concepts.^1674412249415
