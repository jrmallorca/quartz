# Negative Reward Prediction Error (RPE)

A negative [[reward-prediction-error-rpe]] occurs when the actual [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]] is greater than the expected [[reward-in-terms-of-habits]].

This signals that the situation is worse than anticipated, discouraging the behaviour.
