# Achieving muscle hypertrophy

Hypertrophy difference of heavy weights with low volume versus high volume and lower weights are negligible.

Muscles do not recognise the difference between equipment such as free weights and resistance bands, only tension.

This is unlike [[achieving-muscle-strength]] where you must use heavier weights.
