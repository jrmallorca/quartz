# Minimum Information Principle

The minimum information principle mostly relates to [[spaced-repetition]], where questions formulated for [[active-recall]] allow for easier memorisation the simpler they are.

Look at the difference between the following questions for a flashcard:

**Complex**

- What is the difference between an ETF and an individual stock?

**Simple**

- What is an ETF?
- What is a stock?
- In terms of performance, how will an ETF likely perform?
- In terms of performance, how will a stock likely perform?

The complex question is vague and ambiguous. You're more likely to veer off the answer it was looking for, even if they were correct.

The simple questions follow [[atomicity]]; the piece of information is granular enough that it cannot be put simpler. This makes it easy to memorise what has been learned.
