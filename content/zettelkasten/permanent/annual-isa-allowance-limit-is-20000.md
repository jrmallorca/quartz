# Annual ISA allowance limit is £20,000

The yearly total ISA allowance limit for each person is £20,000. Consider the following example:

A person has 3 ISAs: a cash ISA, a cash LISA, and a Stocks & Shares ISA. The cumulative value deposited into all 3 ISAs yearly must be less than or equal to £20,000.
