# Frequency is more important than time with habit formation

With [[habit]] formation, the frequency at which an action is performed is a more important factor than time spent.

Rather than asking

> How long will it take to build a new habit?

Instead ask

> How many repetitions will it take to build a new habit?

Think in terms of [[the-most-effective-lessons-come-from-action-not-planning]] and [[classical-pavlovian-conditioning]].
