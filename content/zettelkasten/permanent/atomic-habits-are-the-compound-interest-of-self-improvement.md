# Atomic habits are the compound interest of self-improvement

[[atomic-habits]] are the [[compound-interest]] of self-improvement.

Making a choice that is 1 percent better or 1 percent worse seems insignificant in the moment, but over the span of moments that make up a lifetime these choices determine the difference between who you are and who you could be. An example is a [[gateway-habit]].

Success is the product of daily habits, not once-in-a-lifetime transformations.
