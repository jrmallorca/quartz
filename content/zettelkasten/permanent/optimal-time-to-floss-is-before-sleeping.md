# Optimal time to floss is before sleeping

It is better to floss before sleeping as [[sugar-causes-tooth-decay-when-cavity-causing-bacteria-are-present]]. As long as any food particles are present within the mouth, it will continually cause decay for as long as you sleep, wake up, and not floss and brush teeth.
