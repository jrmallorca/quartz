# Food with low dynamic contrast can contribute to quicker satiety

In contrast to [[food-with-high-dynamic-contrast-are-superstimuli]], a low [[dynamic-contrast-in-food]] will be boring.

As it's boring, the brain will lose interest faster and contribute as a factor in becoming "fuller" quicker. It leads to quicker [[sensory-specific-satiety]].
