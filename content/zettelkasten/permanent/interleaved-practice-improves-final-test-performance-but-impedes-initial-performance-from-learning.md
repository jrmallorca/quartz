# Interleaved practice improves final test performance but impedes initial performance from learning

[[interleaved-practice]] improves final test performance but impedes initial performance during learning.

In a study from [Rohrer, Doug & Taylor, Kelli. (2007). The shuﬄing of mathematics problems improves learning. Instructional Science. 35. 481-498. 10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8. ](http://dx.doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8), the mixing of problem types, which boosted final test performance by a remarkable 215 percent, actually impeded performance during initial learning.
