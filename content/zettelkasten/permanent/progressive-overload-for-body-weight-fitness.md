# Progressive overload for body-weight fitness

As [[progressive-overload-for-body-weight-fitness-harder-than-strength-training]] and [[progressive-overload-for-body-weight-exercises-are-more-intense-than-weights]], we must bridge the gap through rep progression or resistance bands from the [[effectiveness-of-resistance-bands]].

Once you are satisfied with your current sets and reps, you can move on to the harder exercise or [[correct-technique-in-exercise]] with a lower rep range.

For example, when you reach your maximum sets and reps that you can easily do (e.g., 3 \* 12), then progress onto the minimum sets and reps (e.g., 3 \* 5).
