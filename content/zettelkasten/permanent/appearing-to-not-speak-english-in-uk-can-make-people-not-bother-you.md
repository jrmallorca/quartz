# Appearing to not speak English in UK can make people not bother you

An observation made during university is that those on the street who would be asking people for either their time or money are less likely to approach international students.

Creating an appearance of an identity that does not speak English in the UK will make people less likely to approach you for either your time or money.

These appearances could be the following:

- international student,
- Asian in a predominantly Asian group of friends.
