# Acquiring an agreement in principle

To acquire an [[agreement-in-principle]], you could apply for it either through a mortgage lender directly or via a mortgage broker/adviser. As an [[agreement-in-principle-is-not-a-mortgage-offer]], only the following information will be required:

- Name
- Date of birth
- 3 years of address history
- Income and expenditure

Providing the information MAY not need supporting documents, unlike a full mortgage application.
