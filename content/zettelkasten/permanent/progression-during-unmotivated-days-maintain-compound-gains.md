# Progression during unmotivated days maintain compound gains

Attempting to progress during unmotivated days, even if it's an easier version of the [[habit]], will maintain any compound gains accrued from previous good days.

[[gateway-habit]]s can alleviate this as it lowers the effort required to start the [[habit]].

This will at least continue the [[streak]]s and any benefits from [[good-habits-are-feedback-loops-for-achieving-desired-results-reinforcing-identity-and-improving-self-esteem]].
