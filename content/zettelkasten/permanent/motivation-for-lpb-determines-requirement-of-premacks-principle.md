# Motivation for LPB determines requirement of Premack's principle

The motivation/intrinsic enjoyment for the [[lpb-in-premacks-principle]] determines if [[premacks-principle]] is even needed.

Consider the 1st example in [[premacks-principle-is-also-known-as-grandmas-rule]]:

- To have 1 hour of gaming ([[mpb-in-premacks-principle]]), you must complete your homework ([[lpb-in-premacks-principle]]).

However, if the [[lpb-in-premacks-principle]] is motivating or entertaining enough by itself, the association with the [[mpb-in-premacks-principle]] may not be required. It may even be a [[mpb-in-premacks-principle]] itself.
