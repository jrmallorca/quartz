# Spraying as method of application for toner

Spraying toners will reduce the amount of product use per application while applying toner evenly across the face. The following reasons explain why:

- Applying toner through cotton pads requires the use of cotton pads as a physical exfoliator and may absorb too much toner for appropriate application on the face.
- Applying toner through hands may absorb the toner into the hands rather than the face, requiring more product to apply the appropriate amount to the face.

To make spraying as the method of application, decant the toners into spray bottles.
