# Chroma dimension in colour season

The chroma dimension of the [[colour-season]] determines the saturation of the colours present in the skin, hair, and eyes of the face.

In terms of people, this would determine how contrasted or blended the skin, hair, and eyes are against each other. Those with blended (muted) appearances have a "greyed out" look.
