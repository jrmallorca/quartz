# Habit - Temper wok

## [[cue-in-terms-of-habits|Cues]]

- Before first use.

## [[response-in-terms-of-habits|Responses]]

1. Wash off all machine oil with soap.
2. Dry wok.
3. Heat the metal with the highest heat available.
4. Observe the "bluing" of the carbon steel.
5. Slowly turn the wok until every part of the wok has been tempered.
6. Let the wok cool down slowly.
7. [[habit-season-wok]]
