# Applying facial skincare with palms will share product with hands

Applying facial skincare products through the palms will share the product with the skin of the hands. It may require more product to evenly apply the skincare across the face.

This isn't necessarily bad as it will also moisturise the hands and prevent early aging.

This may be a problem if the product is:

- Expensive.
- Dedicated for the face.
