# Loan-to-value ratio calculation

According to [this article](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/l/loantovalue.asp), [[loan-to-value-ratio]] can be calculated through the following formula:

$$
\begin{align}
MA  &= \text{Mortgage amount} \\
APV &= \text{Appraised property value} \\
\\
LTV ratio &= \frac{MA}{APV}
\end{align}
$$
