# Principle of least effort (Zipf's Law)

The law of least effort states that humans tend to gravitate towards options that requires the least amount of work.

The Zipf's Law part of this is relating the principle to human communications.

For example:

- Instead of foraging for food, or grocery shopping, you get cooked food delivered to you.
- Instead of finding information about a topic through Google/Semantic Scholar, you use Wikipedia even if its information is not the most accurate.
- Instead of writing out whole words, we would use acronyms or emojis. "A picture is worth a 1000 words" after all.
