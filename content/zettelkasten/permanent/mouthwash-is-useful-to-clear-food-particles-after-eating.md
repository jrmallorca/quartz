# Mouthwash is useful to clear food particles after eating

Mouthwash is effective to clear food particles that is stuck in the mouth by rinsing the mouths and stimulating saliva production.

## Analogy

The reason that we use floss is to remove the food particles, especially [[optimal-time-to-floss-is-before-sleeping]]. This is to reduce the amount of time for sugar to decay our teeth: [[dental-plaque-starts-to-build-up-on-teeth-after-20-minutes-of-consuming-sugar]].
