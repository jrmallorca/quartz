# Difference between equity and equality

Equality ensures that all people should be treated the same. Equity takes into consideration of a person's unique circumstances, adjusting treatment accordingly so that the end result is equal.

## Analogy

## Concrete example

Imagine a child and an adult were trying to reach for an apple above them.

Equality means that both the child and adult would have an equal height ladder to reach the apple, though the child would likely still be unable to due to their height.

Equity means that the child will have higher ladder than the adult's such that both of them can reach for the apple.
