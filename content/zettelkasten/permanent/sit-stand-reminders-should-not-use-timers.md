# Sit-stand reminders should not use timers

Reminders to either sit or stand should minimise the use of timers as [[timers-can-disrupt-focus]].

Alternatively, we could do the following:

- Drink frequently to have a natural reminder to take a (pee) break.
- Use boredom or loss of productivity as a [[cue-in-terms-of-habits]] to sit or stand.
