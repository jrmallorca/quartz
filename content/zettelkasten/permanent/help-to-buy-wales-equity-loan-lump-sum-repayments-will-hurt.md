# Help to Buy - Wales equity loan lump sum repayments will hurt

The lump sum payments are the potential deal breaker of the [[help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]]. While the example provided in [[disadvantages-of-help-to-buy-wales-equity-loan]] is a bit unrealistic, can you really afford to save up and pay off AT LEAST ~£30,000 ALL AT ONCE, let alone £60,000, within 10 or so years?
