# Fruit should be stored separately to vegetables

Fruit should be stored separately to vegetables because it can produce ethylene, a natural gas that can over-ripen other fresh food when trapped in a confined space.

Therefore, [[fruit-should-be-stored-at-the-drawer-or-middle-shelves-of-the-fridge]] and [[vegetables-should-be-stored-at-the-drawer-of-the-fridge]].
