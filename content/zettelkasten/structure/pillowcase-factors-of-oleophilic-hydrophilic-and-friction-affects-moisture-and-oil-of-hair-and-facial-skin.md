# Pillowcase factors of oleophilic, hydrophilic, and friction affects moisture and oil of hair and skin

When sleeping, 3 factors can affect the moisture and oil of the hair and facial skin from the pillowcase:

- Oleophilicity (i.e., how absorbent the pillowcase is with oil.)
- Hydrophilicity (i.e., how absorbent the pillowcase is with water.)
- Friction

Ideally, we want all 3 factors to be as low as they possibly can. The ideal pillowcase would be oleophobic, hydrophobic, and frictionless as it would prevent the removal of moisture and oil from the hair and skin.
