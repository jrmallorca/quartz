# Classical/Pavlovian conditioning

Classical/Pavlovian conditioning refers to a [[neutral-stimulus]] becoming a [[conditioned-stimulus]], after being repeatedly associated with an [[unconditioned-stimulus]], which provokes a [[conditioned-response]].
