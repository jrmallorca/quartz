# Impart smoky flavour to food while cooking indoors

Imparting smoky flavours to food typically requires cooking outdoors due to the requirement of cooking food directly on the flame or through smoke. Both these methods are typically impractical to cook indoors due to fire safety.

However, there are still methods to add smoky flavours to food while cooking indoors.
