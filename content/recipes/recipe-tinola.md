# Recipe - Tinola

## Ingredients

- 6-8 chicken drumsticks or thighs.
- 2 green papaya wedged, if available.
- A whole bag of spinach. Alternatively:
    - 1.5 cup malunggay leaves.
    - 1 cup hot pepper leaves.
- 5 cloves chopped garlic.
- 1 chopped onion.
- 1 thumb of ginger, grated into paste or diced.
- 2 chicken stock cubes.
- Enough water to cover the chicken in a pot.
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- Ground black pepper to taste.
- 3 tablespoons olive oil.

## Instructions

### Preparation

1. Cut green papaya into wedges.
2. Chop garlic.
3. If ginger cannot be grated, dice ginger. Otherwise, DO NOT grate ginger yet.
3. Cut onion into either squares or strips. 
4. Salt the onions and chicken.

### Cooking

1. Heat the pot on the stove on medium-low heat until the [[leidenfrost-effect]] is observed.
    - [[habit-wait-for-leidenfrost-effect-to-occur-before-cooking-on-stainless-steel-cookware]].
2. Adjust heat such that it is low enough that the oil won't smoke or burn the garlic while still frying.
3. Add oil into the pot.
3. Add onion, garlic, and ginger if diced. Sauté them until the onion is soft.
4. Increase heat to medium and add the chicken. Sauté until the skin turns light brown in colour.
5. Pour 2 tablespoons of fish sauce and stir until most ingredients are covered in it.
6. Pour enough water into the pot until all of the chicken in covered.
7. If ginger was not diced, grate ginger into a paste and add it into the water.
8. Let the water boil then simmer for 10 minutes on low heat. Add water when chicken is visibly not covered in water.
9. Add the green papaya wedges into the pot and continue to simmer for 15 to 20 minutes.
10. Add the chicken stock cubes, season with ground black pepper and stir.
11. Turn the heat off and add the spinach, or malungay and hot pepper leaves, with the cover still on for 2 minutes.
12. Serve.
