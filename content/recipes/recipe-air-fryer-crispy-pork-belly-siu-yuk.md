# Recipe - Air fryer crispy pork belly (Siu yuk)

## Ingredients

- 1 pork belly.
- Olive oil.
- Coarse salt.
- Five spice powder.
- Pepper.

## Cooking

1. With a clean kitchen towel, pat pork belly dry.
2. For a day, store pork belly in the fridge on a plate or in a container **uncovered**.
3. Place foil on cutting board to eventually shape into a tight box for the pork belly.
4. Place pork belly on the middle of the foil, skin side down.
5. (Optional) [[gashing-food-for-marinades]].
6. Rub salt, pepper, and five spice powder all over the flesh.
7. Flip belly skin side up.
8. (Optional) Pat skin dry again.
9. Rub oil and salt on the skin.
10. Cook in the air fryer for 120°C for 1 hour. 
11. Open air fryer and tighten the foil.
12. Cook in the air fryer for 120°C for 1 hour. 
13. Cook in the air fryer for 240°C for 20 minutes. 
14. [[rest-large-pieces-of-meat-after-cooking]].
15. Serve.

## Notes

- The reason we don't need to pierce the skin is because the olive oil we rub on the skin acts as the fat that fries the skin, rather than the fat from the pork belly.
- There is too much risk when piercing or gashing the skin as we can potentially go too deep and hit the flesh. Once it hits the flesh, the juices will leak out of it.
- If the skin is not horizontally level when cooking in the air fryer, you can make little balls of foil to prop up sides where it's too low.
