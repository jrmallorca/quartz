# Recipe - Boil eggs

## Ingredients

- Eggs.

## Instructions

1. Put (optionally boiling) water into pot.
2. Wait until water is boiling.
3. Gently place the eggs in the boiling water.
4. Depending on the consistency of the egg you want:
    - Boil for 6 minutes for a jam-like consistency.
    - Boil for 7 minutes for a fudge-like consistency.
5. If not eating instantly after, place the eggs on a sieve and run cold water on it.
6. Serve.
