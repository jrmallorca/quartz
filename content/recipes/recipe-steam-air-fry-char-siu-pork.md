# Recipe - (Steam) Air fry char siu pork

## Ingredients

- 1 [[pork]] shoulder.
- Char siu marinade.
	- 1/3 of the Lee Kum Kee char siu marinade jar.
- 2 teaspoons honey.
- (Optional) 125ml water.

## Instructions

1. Gash the pork.
2. Put the marinade into a ziploc bag.
3. Put the pork shoulder into the bag with marinade.
4. Let it marinate for 4-24 hours.
5. Set the air fryer at 200°C.
6. (Optional) put the water just below the pork without touching it.
7. Air fry the pork for 10 minutes.
8. Baste with honey and air fry for 1 minute.
9. Flip the pork and air fry for another 10 minutes.
10. Baste with honey and air fry for 1 minute.
11. Remove the meat onto a clean wooden board.
12. (Optional) attempt to pour the juices from the air fryer onto the meat.
13. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
14. Slice it into 1mm-2mm slices.
15. Serve.
