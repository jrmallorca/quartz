# Recipe - Matcha tea

## Ingredients

- 1 heaped teaspoon of matcha powder.
- ~200ml of boiling water.
    - From the above, 70ml of hot, not boiling, water (70°C-80°C).

## Equipment

1. Matcha bowl (chawan)
2. Whisk (chasu)
3. Sieve
4. Teaspoon (chashaku)
5. Kettle

## Steps

1. Boil water with the kettle.
2. Pour boiling water from the kettle into bowl.
3. Soak the tines of the whisk into the bowl of hot water for 60 seconds.
4. Remove whisk and either put it back on its stand or let it stand on its handle. 
5. Remove hot water and wipe the bowl with a dry cloth.
6. Sift a heaped teaspoon of matcha powder into the bowl.
7. Pour ~70ml of the cooled down hot water from the kettle into the bowl.
8. Slowly circle the whisk inside the bowl to gather the powder.
9. With your wrist, vigorously whisk the inside of the bowl in a back-and-forth motion. With your elbow, slowly move side-to-side while whisking. Do this for 15-30 seconds until a fine foam is observed.
10. Remove whisk and rinse it.
11. Enjoy drinking the matcha straight from the bowl or as an ingredient to another drink.
