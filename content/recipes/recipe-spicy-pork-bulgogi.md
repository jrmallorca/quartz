# Recipe - Spicy Pork Bulgogi

## Ingredients

- 1 pork shoulder or 1 pork belly.
- [[habit-velvet-pork#ingredients]]

- Spicy pork bulgogi marinade/sauce
  - 100 g red apple (e.g. royal gala) or nashi pear
  - 50 g brown onion
  - 3 Tbsp gochujang (Korean chilli paste)
  - 3 Tbsp soy sauce
  - 3 Tbsp honey (or brown sugar)
  - 2 Tbsp gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes)
  - 2 Tbsp rice wine (mirin)
  - 1 Tbsp minced garlic
  - 1 Tbsp minced ginger
  - 1/8 tsp ground black pepper

- A high smoke-point cooking oil/fat.
  - [[cook-with-fat-that-does-not-come-from-seed-oils]]
- 1 onion.
- 2 spring onions.
- (Optional) sesame seeds.

## Instructions

### Preparation

1. Split the spring onions between the white and the green.
2. For each white and green part of the spring onions:
  1. Cut into many pieces.
  2. Place into a small container for later application.
3. Slice the pork against the grain into roughly 1mm - 2mm slices.
4. [[habit-velvet-pork]]

### Cooking

1. Sear pork and place into another container.
2. On low heat, add white part of green onions into wok and cook until aromatic (less than 30 seconds).
3. On medium heat, add onions into wok and cook until slightly soft and translucent.
4. On high heat, add meat back into wok.
5. Add sauce on the sides of the wok.
6. Toss the wok until meat is covered in sauce and completely cooked.
7. Turn heat off.
8. Garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds.
9. Serve.
