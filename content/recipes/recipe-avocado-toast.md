# Recipe - Avocado toast

## Ingredients

- Olive oil.
- Salt.
- Pepper.
- 1-2 bread slices.
- 0.5 avocado, sliced or mashed.
- 1-2 bacon slices.
- 1 egg.
- (Optional) salad leaves.
- (Optional) ingredients for deglazing:
  - 100ml wine.
  - A few drops of lemon juice or vinegar.
  - (Optional) honey.

## Instructions

### Preparation

1. [[habit-wait-for-leidenfrost-effect-to-occur-before-cooking-on-stainless-steel-cookware]]
2. Halve an avocado and slice it lengthways.

### Cooking

1. On low heat, add oil into pan and fry one side of the bread slices until golden.
2. Flip the bread slices, add more olive oil, and continue frying until golden.
3. Take out the bread slices onto a plate and fry the bacon. While it's not cooked yet:
  1. (Optional) spread salad leaves on bread.
  2. Spread avocado on bread.
4. Add bacon onto bread.
5. (Optional) deglaze the pan:
  1. Add wine into pan and scrub the fond back into the liquid.
  2. Add a bit of lemon or vinegar.
  3. If it's too sour, add a bit of honey.
6. Add more oil into pan.
7. Crack the egg into the pan.
8. Season the egg with salt, pepper, and other desired seasoning.
9. If there is excess oil, angle the pan and spoon the oil onto the top of the egg.
10. Place the cooked egg on top of the bread.
11. (Optional) add the deglazed sauce on top of the bread.
12. Serve.
